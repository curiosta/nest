Live events & specialised hardware

Drone light shows, robot dance crews, kinetic installs, and live AV/event tech need humans on site: rigging, swarm ops, safety perimeters, show-call timing, and hardware that fails in weather. Agentic tools can storyboard or cue lists; they cannot yet replace the crew that flies, dances, hangs, and troubleshoots under a live audience.

Drone shows stay here. Drone survey and mapping work is under Survey & geospatial field.

How leaders run their tech, and what it changes

How listed employers run ops tech, automation, safety systems and workforce skilling in this physical domain, and what that changes for roles and teams. Notes prefer official strategy / annual-report / IR / careers pages. TBC means not verified this pass — we never invent. Each “What it changes” line is our reading of the cited note (NEST view), placed on the AI role ladder.

BotLab Dynamics

India-based drone light-show & swarm hardware employer (show ops + R&D)

How they run their techOfficial about page: Make-in-India swarm stack — in-house flight controllers, ESC, RTK GPS, ELRS receivers, firmware and nano-drone hardware; claimed 10,000+ fleet capacity and 350,000+ flight hours; multi-city national and international outdoor shows. Ground crews and swarm ops remain physical under live audiences.

Source: BotLab Dynamics — About Us · checked 5 Oct 2026

What it changesDrone shows need pilots, technicians and choreographers; the fleet runs on software, so one show crew orchestrates hundreds of drones (L2). NEST view

HARMAN Professional Solutions

Global pro AV / lighting / control brand with India & APAC go-to-market (live + install)

How they run their techIndia/APAC leadership interviews and PALM Expo coverage: focus on AV-over-IP, immersive / spatial audio (e.g. FLUX:: SPAT), Martin Professional lighting, AMX MUSE open control, and integrated Live Arena demos — AI-ready control framed as assist for live operators, not a replacement for on-site FOH and lighting crews.

Source: PALM Expo Magazine / India-in-Focus interview (Amar G. Subash) + PALM Expo HARMAN Live Arena notes · checked 5 Oct 2026

What it changesAV-over-IP and open control make AV crews part network technicians; riggers and audio techs who learn networks move up. NEST view

Wizcraft

Large Indian live-events / experiential producer using sound, light, lasers, video & projection

How they run their techOfficial site: integrates strategy, creativity and technology for large ceremonies and brand experiences; special-events pages describe son et lumière / projection-mapping spectaculars and digital/AR event solutions. Exact internal show-control stack beyond marketing pages is not fully enumerated — treat as production + tech ops employer rather than a single OEM.

Source: Wizcraft — official site (special events / digital solutions) · checked 5 Oct 2026

What it changesProjection mapping and AR add digital content roles next to stage, rigging and lighting crews; producers coordinate both (L2). NEST view

Absen

Global LED display OEM active in India rental/staging and Pro AV channels

How they run their techOfficial product and India market pages: rental LED series (e.g. NT V2) for corporate events, exhibitions, broadcast rental and xR stages; 2025 India CXO Summit outlined Pro AV roadmap (KLCOB V2, CPS, A25, X V2). Local rental partners handle install/strike — physical crew work stays central.

Source: Absen — NT V2 rental series + 2025 India CXO Summit · checked 5 Oct 2026

What it changesRental LED walls need crews who rig, calibrate and repair panels; rental-partner technicians become the skilled rung. NEST view

Levels in this domain

Roles here are evolving along the AI role ladder. Most people start at L1, Intelligent Proxy: the trained person on site who uses expert-written AI skills and stays accountable for the physical work.

Example roles at each rung. NEST view

  1. L1 Intelligent ProxyRigger using a load-check skill before a show
  2. L2 OrchestratorProduction manager running agents for cue sheets, crew calls and kit lists
  3. L3 Skill developerSenior technician writing a drone-show pre-flight skill
  4. L4 Harness and model builderDrone-show engineer building swarm flight controllers and firmware. Example: BotLab Dynamics' in-house flight controllers and firmware
