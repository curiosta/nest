Live events & specialised hardware

Drone light shows, robot dance crews, kinetic installs, and live AV/event tech need humans on site: rigging, swarm ops, safety perimeters, show-call timing, and hardware that fails in weather. Agentic tools can storyboard or cue lists; they cannot yet replace the crew that flies, dances, hangs, and troubleshoots under a live audience.

Drone shows stay here. Drone survey and mapping work is under Survey & geospatial field.

Start here

Shows run on software and drone swarms, but riggers and pilots still hold the safety line. Most people enter as AV assistant, LED wall loader, drone ground crew trainee. The routes below get you to L1; the ladder underneath shows how the work changes at each rung. NEST view

L1 These routes prepare you for L1, Intelligent Proxy. The next rung, L2 Orchestrator, needs time on the job running a crew's work plus several AI tools. NEST view

  • ITI trade (DGT CTS list): Electrician, Video Cameraman, Photographer.
  • Apprenticeship: NAPS, through apprenticeshipindia.gov.in (PIB).
  • Job fairs and career centres: National Career Service (NCS).
  • Skill council course: Media and Entertainment Skills Council (MESC); their qualification packs (QPs) are listed on the National Qualification Register (NQR). Qualifications on the NQR for this work include Sound Engineer (NSQF level 4); Gaffar Man (NSQF level 5). Check that a training centre is accredited for the exact qualification before you enrol.

Verified fairs tagged to this domain: 6. Typical progression on this page is labelled NEST view; we give no salaries.

Levels in this domain

  1. L1Intelligent ProxyUses expert AI skills; does the physical work
  2. L2OrchestratorRuns several AI tools and a crew's workflow
  3. L3Skill developerWrites the skills others use
  4. L4Harness and model builderBuilds the tools skills run on

Roles here are evolving along the AI role ladder. Most people start at L1, Intelligent Proxy: the trained person on site who uses expert-written AI skills and stays accountable for the physical work.

Example roles at each rung. NEST view

What each rung looks like here

Composite sketches, not real people, grounded in the tools and employers on this page. NEST view

L1 Intelligent Proxy: a day at this rung

Rigger. This person loads a load-check skill before a show. On the job, this person still rigs the truss and checks every shackle. Where they stop and consult: load doubts go to the head rigger; flying drones needs the licensed remote pilot. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L2 Orchestrator: a day at this rung

Production manager. This person loads agents for cue sheets, crew calls and kit lists. On the job, this person still walks the stage before doors open. Where they stop and consult: safety calls go to the venue safety officer. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L3 Skill developer: a day at this rung

Senior technician. This person loads a drone-show pre-flight skill. On the job, this person still runs pre-flight checks on real batteries and drones. Where they stop and consult: flight permissions follow DGCA rules and the licensed pilot in charge. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L4 Harness and model builder: a day at this rung

Drone-show engineer. This person loads swarm flight controllers and firmware. On the job, this person still tests drones on the field with the show crew. Where they stop and consult: nothing flies over a crowd without the pilot in charge approving it. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. BotLab Dynamics

India-based drone light-show & swarm hardware employer (show ops + R&D)

How they run their techOfficial about page: Make-in-India swarm stack — in-house flight controllers, ESC, RTK GPS, ELRS receivers, firmware and nano-drone hardware; claimed 10,000+ fleet capacity and 350,000+ flight hours; multi-city national and international outdoor shows. Ground crews and swarm ops remain physical under live audiences.

Source: BotLab Dynamics — About Us · checked 5 Oct 2026

What it changesDrone shows need pilots, technicians and choreographers; the fleet runs on software, so one show crew orchestrates hundreds of drones (L2). NEST view

HARMAN Professional Solutions

Global pro AV / lighting / control brand with India & APAC go-to-market (live + install)

How they run their techIndia/APAC leadership interviews and PALM Expo coverage: focus on AV-over-IP, immersive / spatial audio (e.g. FLUX:: SPAT), Martin Professional lighting, AMX MUSE open control, and integrated Live Arena demos — AI-ready control framed as assist for live operators, not a replacement for on-site FOH and lighting crews.

Source: PALM Expo Magazine / India-in-Focus interview (Amar G. Subash) + PALM Expo HARMAN Live Arena notes · checked 5 Oct 2026

What it changesAV-over-IP and open control make AV crews part network technicians; riggers and audio techs who learn networks move up. NEST view
