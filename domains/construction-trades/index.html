Skilled construction & trades

On-site judgment, code compliance, and fine motor work in messy environments resist desk automation. Agentic tools may plan or estimate, but the physical install still needs a craftsperson.

Fit-out and running of server halls (electrical, HVAC and fibre crews) now sits under Data centres & cooling. Building the shell stays here.

How leaders run their tech, and what it changes

How listed employers run ops tech, automation, safety systems and workforce skilling in this physical domain, and what that changes for roles and teams. Notes prefer official strategy / annual-report / IR / careers pages. TBC means not verified this pass — we never invent. Each “What it changes” line is our reading of the cited note (NEST view), placed on the AI role ladder.

Larsen & Toubro (L&T)

India’s leading engineering & construction employer for site trades

How they run their techLakshya 2031 / FY25–26: tech-driven E&C — BIM, IoT (17,000+ assets), drones/LiDAR, Industry 4.0 shop robots/cobots, digital field ops (mobile workforce apps, VR-based safety training, smart-glass remote inspection), AI/GenAI across tendering–execution; Industry 5.0 human–machine collaboration priorities.

Source: L&T Integrated Annual Report FY2025–26 (Digital Transformation PDF) · checked 6 Oct 2026

What it changesSite crews get apps, VR training and remote inspection, so some inspection and supervision moves off site; engineers who encode procedures into digital workflows move up to L3. NEST view

Tata Projects

Large EPC employer for infrastructure / industrial site trades

How they run their techFY2024–25 integrated report: digital-first execution across sites using BIM, IoT, AI and digital twins for predictability, safety and low-carbon delivery; multi-year Phoenix transformation programme covering culture, processes and systems.

Source: Tata Projects Integrated Annual Report FY2024–25 · checked 6 Oct 2026

What it changesBIM and digital twins pull planning and coordination roles together; site engineers increasingly orchestrate digital tools and contractors (L2) rather than chase paper. NEST view

NBCC (India)

Navratna CPSE PMC/EPC employer on government construction programmes

How they run their techFY2024–25 / FY2025–26 reports: real-time project monitoring, ERP upgrades, digital procurement and document automation; wider BIM adoption, Vendor Portal, Office 365 collaboration, AI-into-operations and cybersecurity investments across the project lifecycle.

Source: NBCC Integrated Annual Report (digital / technological transformation) · checked 6 Oct 2026

What it changesMonitoring, procurement and documents move online, so clerical and follow-up tasks shrink and project staff spend more time on oversight of contractors (L2). NEST view

Levels in this domain

  1. L1Intelligent ProxyUses expert AI skills; does the physical work
  2. L2OrchestratorRuns several AI tools and a crew's workflow
  3. L3Skill developerWrites the skills others use
  4. L4Harness and model builderBuilds the tools skills run on

Roles here are evolving along the AI role ladder. Most people start at L1, Intelligent Proxy: the trained person on site who uses expert-written AI skills and stays accountable for the physical work.

Example roles at each rung. NEST view

  1. L1 Intelligent ProxySite supervisor using a daily safety-check skill on a phone Example: L&T site crews use mobile workforce apps, VR safety training and smart-glass remote inspection
  2. L2 OrchestratorSite engineer running agents for material take-offs, schedules and snag lists Example: Tata Projects runs sites with BIM, IoT, AI and digital twins together
  3. L3 Skill developerExperienced engineer writing a pour-readiness inspection skill NEST view
  4. L4 Harness and model builderConstruction-tech engineer building AI and GenAI tools on BIM, drone and site data, such as progress tracking and safety-image checks. Example: L&T's digital, AI and IT programme
