Survey & geospatial field

Before anything is built, mapped or titled, someone walks the land with a GNSS rover, flies a licensed drone or keeps a reference station running. Software processes the data; the surveyor collects it and signs it off.

Drone shows stay under Live events & specialised hardware; crop-spraying drones sit with Agri / food processing / cold chain.

Start here

Mapping software works fast, but only with field data someone collected correctly on site. Most people enter as survey helper or chainman, drone-pilot trainee. The routes below get you to L1; the ladder underneath shows how the work changes at each rung. NEST view

L1 These routes prepare you for L1, Intelligent Proxy. The next rung, L2 Orchestrator, needs time on the job running a crew's work plus several AI tools. NEST view

  • ITI trade (DGT CTS list): Surveyor, Draughtsman (Civil), Geo-Informatics Assistant, Drone Pilot (Junior).
  • Apprenticeship: NAPS, through apprenticeshipindia.gov.in (PIB).
  • Job fairs and career centres: National Career Service (NCS).
  • Skill council course: Aerospace and Aviation Sector Skill Council (AASSC) and Construction Skill Development Council of India (CSDCI); their qualification packs (QPs) are listed on the National Qualification Register (NQR). Qualifications on the NQR for this work include Assistant Surveyor (NSQF level 2); Surveyor (NSQF level 5.5). Check that a training centre is accredited for the exact qualification before you enrol.

Roles here are evolving along the AI role ladder. Most people start at L1, Intelligent Proxy: the trained person on site who uses expert-written AI skills and stays accountable for the physical work.

What each rung looks like here

Composite sketches, not real people, grounded in the tools and employers on this page. NEST view

L1 Intelligent Proxy: a day at this rung

Surveyor. This person loads a field-checklist skill for a GNSS or drone job. On the job, this person still sets up the rover and flies within the rules. Where they stop and consult: airspace and permission questions go to the licensed remote pilot. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L2 Orchestrator: a day at this rung

Survey lead. This person loads agents for flight plans, processing and QC. On the job, this person still checks ground control in the field. Where they stop and consult: legal boundary disputes go to the revenue authorities. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L3 Skill developer: a day at this rung

Senior surveyor. This person loads a ground-control skill. On the job, this person still places and checks control points to test it. Where they stop and consult: methods are checked against survey standards. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L4 Harness and model builder: a day at this rung

Geospatial-AI engineer. This person loads feature-extraction and mapping models on LiDAR and drone imagery. On the job, this person still goes to the field to check ground truth. Where they stop and consult: maps for legal use are certified by licensed surveyors. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

Employer notes: how leaders run their tech, and what it changes

How leaders run their tech, and what it changes

How listed employers run ops tech, automation, safety systems and workforce skilling in this physical domain, and what that changes for roles and teams. Notes prefer official strategy / annual-report / IR / careers pages. TBC means not verified this pass — we never invent. Each “What it changes” line is our reading of the cited note (NEST view), placed on the AI role ladder.

Survey of India

National survey and mapping agency

How they run their techOfficial CORS portal: a network of permanent Continuously Operating Reference Stations streams satellite observations to a central server and provides real-time positioning (RTK / NRTK) to about ±3 cm, so field surveyors don't need their own base station.

Source: Survey of India CORS portal — introduction · checked 11 Oct 2026

What it changesCORS replaces a base station for each crew, so a surveyor with a rover works alone; field crews shrink and survey checks move to the network. NEST view

Genesys International

Mapping company with field mobile-mapping and LiDAR crews

How they run their techOfficial LiDAR page: India's largest fleet of mobile-mapping units, with terrestrial, backpack, mobile and aerial LiDAR scanners; airborne surveys with partner fixed-wing, UAV and helicopter operators.

Source: Genesys International — LiDAR engineering · checked 11 Oct 2026

What it changesMobile and aerial LiDAR replace much manual field measurement; field crews operate scanners, and processing teams become the bigger group. NEST view

ideaForge

Drone maker whose UAVs are used for mapping, surveying and inspection

How they run their techAnnual Report 2024-25: photogrammetry, LiDAR and multispectral payloads; iFCare 360, described as the first subscription-based support service in the Indian UAV industry; after-sales training and product support.

Source: ideaForge Annual Report 2024-25 (exchange filing) · checked 11 Oct 2026

What it changesDrone payloads turn surveyors and inspectors into drone operators; after-sales trainers and support technicians become new roles. NEST view

Garuda Aerospace

Drone maker and DGCA-approved Remote Pilot Training Organisation

How they run their techOfficial RPTO case study: training runs from ground theory to simulation to live flying; 2,542 certified drone pilots trained across centres in 26 cities by 2024, with 325 institution MoUs; courses include a drone-technician track.

Source: Garuda Aerospace — DGCA-approved RPTO · checked 11 Oct 2026

What it changesDrone training creates pilots and technicians as new trades; trainers and examiners are the senior rung. NEST view

Human in the loop here

Survey drones fly themselves for much of the job, but a licensed remote pilot is in charge of every flight. NEST view These are Human in the Loop roles: people who supervise, correct, approve or take over from AI and robots. All HITL roles and how they map to L1–L4 →

  • Drone pilot: Flies survey, farm-spraying, inspection or show drones, watches the autopilot and takes manual control when needed. NEST view
    Evidence: PIB, Drone Rules 2021: DGCA oversees drone schools; the remote pilot licence follows a remote pilot certificate from an authorised drone school; PIB: setting up a Remote Pilot Training Organisation (RPTO) needs DGCA authorisation through the Digital Sky platform

Also: PIB (25 Mar 2026): 42,412 remote pilots trained by 251 DGCA-authorised RPTOs.
