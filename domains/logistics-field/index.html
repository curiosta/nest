Logistics, field & last-mile

Moving goods and serving irregular field sites needs bodies in space. Routing agents help; loading, driving, and doorstep delivery remain physical until autonomy is ubiquitous and legal. NEST view

Fairs tagged Logistics, field All domains

Start here

Software plans the routes and the sorting; loading, driving and the doorstep still need people. Most people enter as picker/packer, loader, delivery associate. The routes below get you to L1; the ladder underneath shows how the work changes at each rung. NEST view

L1 These routes prepare you for L1, Intelligent Proxy. The next rung, L2 Orchestrator, needs time on the job running a crew's work plus several AI tools. NEST view

  • ITI trade (DGT CTS list): Warehouse Technician, In Plant Logistics Assistant, DRIVER CUM MECHANIC (LMV), Mechanic Electric Vehicle.
  • Apprenticeship: NAPS, through apprenticeshipindia.gov.in (PIB).
  • Job fairs and career centres: National Career Service (NCS).
  • Skill council course: Logistics Sector Skill Council and Automotive Skills Development Council (ASDC); their qualification packs (QPs) are listed on the National Qualification Register (NQR). Qualifications on the NQR for this work include Forklift Operator/Driver (NSQF level 3). Check that a training centre is accredited for the exact qualification before you enrol.

Levels in this domain

NEST view The L1–L4 ladder, including the Intelligent Proxy, is NEST Collective’s own lens. What it is

  1. L1Intelligent ProxyUses expert AI skills; does the physical work
  2. L2OrchestratorRuns several AI tools and a crew's workflow
  3. L3Skill developerWrites the skills others use
  4. L4Harness and model builderBuilds the tools skills run on

Roles here are evolving along the AI role ladder. Most people start at L1, Intelligent Proxy: the trained person on site who uses expert-written AI skills and stays accountable for the physical work.

Example roles at each rung. NEST view

  1. L1 Intelligent ProxyDelivery rider using a route and exception-handling skill Example: CONCOR's mobile first- and last-mile app and rake tracking portal
  2. L2 OrchestratorHub supervisor running agents for slotting, staffing and returns Example: Mahindra Logistics' LogiOne links warehouses, transporters and customers in one view
  3. L3 Skill developerOperations expert writing a damaged-shipment handling skill NEST view
  4. L4 Harness and model builderLogistics-AI engineer building agentic transport-management, routing and returns models. Example: Delhivery's agentic TMS and AI tools

What each rung looks like here

Composite sketches, not real people, grounded in the tools and employers on this page. NEST view

L1 Intelligent Proxy: a day at this rung

Delivery rider. This person loads a route and exception-handling skill. On the job, this person still loads the bike, carries parcels and handles the doorstep. Where they stop and consult: damaged or suspicious parcels go back to the hub supervisor. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L2 Orchestrator: a day at this rung

Hub supervisor. This person loads agents for slotting, staffing and returns. On the job, this person still walks the hub floor and fixes what the plan missed. Where they stop and consult: forklift and safety incidents go to the safety officer. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L3 Skill developer: a day at this rung

Operations expert. This person loads a damaged-shipment handling skill. On the job, this person still handles real damaged shipments while testing it. Where they stop and consult: insurance and legal questions go to the claims team. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L4 Harness and model builder: a day at this rung

Logistics-AI engineer. This person loads agentic routing and returns models. On the job, this person still rides along with drivers to see where routes fail. Where they stop and consult: changes that affect driver hours or safety are reviewed by operations heads. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. Employer notes: how leaders run their tech, and what it changes

How leaders run their tech, and what it changes

How listed employers run ops tech, automation, safety systems and workforce skilling in this physical domain, and what that changes for roles and teams. Notes prefer official strategy / annual-report / IR / careers pages. TBC means not verified this pass — we never invent. Each “What it changes” line is our reading of the cited note (NEST view), placed on the AI role ladder.

Delhivery

Large integrated logistics employer (warehouse, line-haul, last-mile)

How they run their techFY26–FY27 shareholder letters/press: ASRS, AMRs, pallet shuttles to de-risk labour availability; AI for demand-shaping, SmartNDR returns, claims/customer support; agentic TMS (TransportOne) and Delhivery Maps from proprietary telemetry — humans supervise exceptions while physical sortation/delivery remains field work.

Source: Delhivery Q1FY27 shareholder letter · checked 6 Oct 2026

What it changesAutomation (ASRS, AMRs) and an agentic transport system reduce manual sorting and routing work; hub staff supervise machines and handle exceptions (L2). NEST view

Blue Dart Express

Major express logistics brand with air hubs & ground ops

How they run their techOfficial press on Delhi/Mumbai air-hub expansion: automated hubs sized for high daily tonnage, with end-to-end routing, sorting and doorstep delivery management under DHL eCommerce investment — automation raises throughput; last-mile remains human.

Source: Blue Dart press — air hub automation investment · checked 6 Oct 2026

What it changesAutomated hubs raise throughput with fewer hands per parcel; last-mile delivery stays human, and hub operators orchestrate sorting systems. NEST view

Mahindra Logistics

3PL / contract logistics employer for warehouse & transport roles

How they run their techFY26 integrated report: LogiOne AI-powered suite for end-to-end visibility connecting warehouses, transporters and customers; real-time visibility and analytics framed as core to integrated operations.

Source: Mahindra Logistics Integrated Annual Report FY26 · checked 6 Oct 2026

What it changesEnd-to-end visibility puts warehouses and transporters on one screen; coordinators shift from phone follow-up to managing exceptions (L2). NEST view

CONCOR (Container Corporation of India)

Rail container logistics PSU employing terminal, yard and cold-chain field roles

How they run their techOfficial investor presentations: AI/ML terminal management and automation, Rake Monitoring Portal (real-time geospatial rake tracking from Jun 2025), mobile first/last-mile app, e-Office / e-billing / e-filing, and Ice Battery reefer cold-chain with live location/temperature track-and-trace.

Source: CONCOR investor presentation (digital / AI terminal management) · checked 6 Oct 2026

What it changesRake tracking and terminal automation move tracking and paperwork online; terminal staff act on system data, and some clerical roles shrink. NEST view
