Teaching & hands-on training

Teaching a child to read, showing an apprentice how to hold a welding torch or helping a child with special needs all need a person in the room who watches, corrects and keeps everyone safe. AI can prepare material; it cannot run the workshop.

Sports and yoga coaches who teach in schools also appear under Sports, fitness & wellness.

Levels in this domain

  1. L1Intelligent ProxyUses expert AI skills; does the physical work
  2. L2OrchestratorRuns several AI tools and a crew's workflow
  3. L3Skill developerWrites the skills others use
  4. L4Harness and model builderBuilds the tools skills run on

Roles here are evolving along the AI role ladder. Most people start at L1, Intelligent Proxy: the trained person on site who uses expert-written AI skills and stays accountable for the physical work.

Example roles at each rung. NEST view

  1. L1 Intelligent ProxyITI instructor using a lesson-plan skill for a workshop session Example: Kidzee introduced new applications to support teacher training
  2. L2 OrchestratorTraining-centre head running agents for timetables, assessments and records Example: Tata STRIVE facilitators blend classroom, digital self-learning and on-the-job learning
  3. L3 Skill developerMaster trainer writing a practical-assessment skill for other instructors Example: DGT's Craft Instructor Training Scheme trains ITI instructors Example: Shiksha Copilot: curators co-create English and Kannada lesson plans with AI, which teachers then adapt for their classes
  4. L4 Harness and model builderTraining-tech developer with instructors, building AI-powered VR and AR simulations of real hazards. Example: L&T's AI-powered VR and AR safety simulations

What each rung looks like here

Composite sketches, not real people, grounded in the tools and employers on this page. NEST view

L1 Intelligent Proxy: a day at this rung

ITI instructor. This person loads a lesson-plan skill for a workshop session. On the job, this person still demonstrates the trade and corrects trainees at the machine. Where they stop and consult: unsafe equipment goes to the workshop head. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L2 Orchestrator: a day at this rung

Training-centre head. This person loads agents for timetables, assessments and records. On the job, this person still observes classes and checks assessment records. Where they stop and consult: certification issues go to the awarding body. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L3 Skill developer: a day at this rung

Master trainer. This person loads a practical-assessment skill for other instructors. On the job, this person still assesses real trainees with it. Where they stop and consult: assessment standards are checked against the official syllabus. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L4 Harness and model builder: a day at this rung

Training-tech developer. This person loads VR and AR simulations of real hazards. On the job, this person still runs sessions with instructors and trainees. Where they stop and consult: simulations are reviewed by trade experts before use. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

Demand signals

  • DGT: about 95,000 instructor positions across about 15,000 ITIs, but only about 15% are CITS-trained; NCVT requires every ITI trainer to be CITS-trained. Source: DGT — Craft Instructor Training Scheme
  • Submissions from 18 states and UTs (Project Approval Board minutes matched with UDISE+): 3,57,862 sanctioned government-school teaching posts vacant, Sep 2025. Source: Careers360 (from PAB minutes and UDISE+)

What we read in this: We read the instructor-training gap as demand for CITS-trained ITI instructors and assessors; demonstrating a trade safely is the bottleneck skill. L1 is the most accessible rung for working instructors in the next 12 to 18 months. NEST view

How roles and teams are shifting

Instructors are becoming guides who mix classroom, simulation and digital self-learning. Trainers who orchestrate these tools work at L2; those who build simulations and courses work at L3. NEST view

L&T Construction Skills Training Institutes

Larsen & Toubro runs ten CSTIs offering free construction-trade courses

How they run their techOfficial technology-for-safety page: L&T uses AI-powered virtual-reality and augmented-reality simulations so workmen can practise emergencies and hazardous conditions without real risk, improving hazard recognition and decisions.

Source: L&T — Technology for safety · checked 11 Oct 2026

What it changesVR and AR simulations change how trainers teach hazards; trainers orchestrate simulations (L2) and senior trainers build scenarios (L3). NEST view

Zee Learn (Kidzee)

Preschool network (Kidzee) and K-12 schools (Mount Litera Zee School)

How they run their techAnnual Report 2024-25: describes Kidzee as India's most widely present preschool network; runs a Kidzee Teacher Training Programme in Child Development and Education; new applications introduced to improve teacher-training support, automation and internal communication.

Source: Zee Learn Annual Report 2024-25 · checked 11 Oct 2026

What it changesTeacher-training apps support preschool teachers; teachers stay in the classroom, and trainers shift towards digital coaching. NEST view

Tata STRIVE

Tata Trusts skilling initiative with its own centres and partner centres

How they run their techOfficial Tata Trusts page: a 'Phygital' model, with instructor-led classroom learning followed by digital self-learning and hands-on learning with an industry partner near the student; facilitators at partner centres complete Tata STRIVE's train-the-trainer courses.

Source: Tata Trusts — Tata STRIVE Skill Development Centre · checked 11 Oct 2026

What it changesA 'phygital' model splits teaching into classroom, digital self-learning and on-the-job learning; facilitators become guides who orchestrate all three (L2). NEST view

Human in the loop here

AI can draft a lesson plan in seconds; teachers and curators decide what reaches the classroom. NEST view These are Human in the Loop roles: people who supervise, correct, approve or take over from AI and robots. All HITL roles and how they map to L1–L4 →

  • Lesson-plan curator and reviewing teacher: Checks AI-drafted lesson plans for accuracy and local fit, edits them, then adapts them for their own class. NEST view
    Evidence: Microsoft Research: Shiksha Copilot in Karnataka government schools; English and Kannada plans co-created by curators and AI, then customised by teachers; Cornell Chronicle (Oct 2026): curators review the plans for accuracy, and teachers further customise them
