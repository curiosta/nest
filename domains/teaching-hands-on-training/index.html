🧑‍🏫 Domain

Teaching & hands-on training

Teaching a child to read, showing an apprentice how to hold a welding torch or helping a child with special needs all need a person in the room who watches, corrects and keeps everyone safe. AI can prepare material; it cannot run the workshop.

Sports and yoga coaches who teach in schools also appear under Sports, fitness & wellness.

Skill ladder

EntryTeaching assistant, preschool helper, lab or workshop attendant
SkilledQualified teacher (TET), ITI instructor, special educator, early-childhood educator, school coach
SeniorHead teacher, department or workshop head, centre head
Trainer / assessorTeacher educator (DIET or B.Ed college), CITS-trained master trainer at an NSTI, SSC-certified trainer (ToT) or assessor (ToA)

Demand signals

  • DGT: about 95,000 instructor positions across about 15,000 ITIs, but only about 15% are CITS-trained; NCVT requires every ITI trainer to be CITS-trained. Source: DGT — Craft Instructor Training Scheme
  • Submissions from 18 states and UTs (Project Approval Board minutes matched with UDISE+): 3,57,862 sanctioned government-school teaching posts vacant, Sep 2025. Source: Careers360 (from PAB minutes and UDISE+)

L&T Construction Skills Training Institutes

Larsen & Toubro runs ten CSTIs offering free construction-trade courses

Current technology managementOfficial technology-for-safety page: L&T uses AI-powered virtual-reality and augmented-reality simulations so workmen can practise emergencies and hazardous conditions without real risk, improving hazard recognition and decisions.

Source: L&T — Technology for safety · checked 11 Oct 2026

Zee Learn (Kidzee)

Preschool network (Kidzee) and K-12 schools (Mount Litera Zee School)

Current technology managementAnnual Report 2024-25: describes Kidzee as India's most widely present preschool network; runs a Kidzee Teacher Training Programme in Child Development and Education; new applications introduced to improve teacher-training support, automation and internal communication.

Tata STRIVE

Tata Trusts skilling initiative with its own centres and partner centres

Current technology managementOfficial Tata Trusts page: a 'Phygital' model, with instructor-led classroom learning followed by digital self-learning and hands-on learning with an industry partner near the student; facilitators at partner centres complete Tata STRIVE's train-the-trainer courses.

Source: Tata Trusts — Tata STRIVE Skill Development Centre · checked 11 Oct 2026

With AI / For AI

With AI

Lesson-planning tools, digital content and simulators help teachers prepare and trainees practise (Zee Learn and L&T; see leaders above). Demonstrating, correcting and keeping a class or workshop safe stays with the teacher. NEST view

For AI

Every new AI-era trade, such as data-centre technician, drone pilot or EV mechanic, needs instructors and assessors in ITIs, NSTIs and training centres. NEST view
