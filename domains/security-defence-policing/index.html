Security, defence & policing

Guarding a gate, patrolling a site, managing a crowd or serving in uniform needs an accountable person on the ground. Cameras and AI analytics watch; people decide and act.

SIS Ltd

India's largest listed security-services employer

How they run their techAnnual Report 2024-25: SISCORE platform for contracts, manpower deployment and billing; iOPS operations platform and QACA compliance audits; MySIS app with facial-recognition attendance, salary alerts, leave and grievances; M-Trainer mobile learning; smart scheduling and patrolling; training academies.

Source: SIS Annual Report 2024-25 · checked 11 Oct 2026

What it changesApps for attendance, pay and training put guards on a digital system; supervisors manage more guards per person through dashboards (L2). NEST view

Checkmate Services

Manned guarding, cash and electronic-security services

How they run their techOfficial services page: guards are police-verified and medically certified, trained in classroom and practice at its own accredited facility plus site-specific training; a command centre for remote monitoring.

Source: Checkmate — services · checked 11 Oct 2026

What it changesA command centre lets fewer staff monitor many sites remotely; guards on site respond to what the centre sees. NEST view

Securitas India

Guarding and electronic-security provider

How they run their techOfficial Security Operations Centre page: trained operators with monitoring technology detect, verify and respond to events around the clock, verifying what is happening before anyone is dispatched.

Source: Securitas India — Security Operations Centre · checked 11 Oct 2026

What it changesOperators in the operations centre verify events before anyone is sent; patrol roles shift towards response, and monitoring becomes its own skilled role. NEST view

How roles and teams are shifting

Command centres and workforce apps let fewer supervisors manage more guards and sites. Guards stay on post and respond; monitoring becomes its own skilled role. NEST view

Levels in this domain

  1. L1Intelligent ProxyUses expert AI skills; does the physical work
  2. L2OrchestratorRuns several AI tools and a crew's workflow
  3. L3Skill developerWrites the skills others use
  4. L4Harness and model builderBuilds the tools skills run on

Roles here are evolving along the AI role ladder. Most people start at L1, Intelligent Proxy: the trained person on site who uses expert-written AI skills and stays accountable for the physical work.

Example roles at each rung. NEST view

  1. L1 Intelligent ProxyGuard using a patrol and incident-report skill Example: SIS guards use the MySIS app for attendance, pay and training
  2. L2 OrchestratorSecurity supervisor running agents for shift plans and incident review Example: Securitas operators watch, verify and dispatch from a Security Operations Centre
  3. L3 Skill developerExperienced officer writing a site-security audit skill Example: Checkmate trains guards at its own accredited facility, with site-specific training
  4. L4 Harness and model builderSecurity-tech engineer building face-recognition, video-analytics and alert-verification models. Example: SIS's MySIS app with face-recognition attendance
