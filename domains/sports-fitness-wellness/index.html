🏋️ Domain

Sports, fitness & wellness

Coaching a swimmer, spotting a lift, leading a trek, teaching yoga or cutting hair all happen hands-on, with a trained person watching your body and your safety. Apps can plan the session; a person runs it.

This domain also covers beauty, salon and spa work. Coaches who teach in schools also appear under Teaching & hands-on training.
