Work with AI

Sports, fitness & wellness

Coaching a swimmer, spotting a lift, leading a trek, teaching yoga or cutting hair all happen hands-on, with a trained person watching your body and your safety. Apps can plan the session; a person runs it.

This domain also covers beauty, salon and spa work. Coaches who teach in schools also appear under Teaching & hands-on training.

Start here

Wearables collect the data; a coach or therapist still has to watch, correct and keep people safe. Most people enter as gym floor trainee, salon or spa assistant, assistant trek guide. The routes below get you to L1; the ladder underneath shows how the work changes at each rung. NEST view

L1 These routes prepare you for L1, Intelligent Proxy. The next rung, L2 Orchestrator, needs time on the job running a crew's work plus several AI tools. NEST view

  • ITI trade (DGT CTS list): Spa Therapy, Cosmetology, Hair & Skin Care, Physiotherapy Technician.
  • Apprenticeship: NAPS, through apprenticeshipindia.gov.in (PIB).
  • Job fairs and career centres: National Career Service (NCS).
  • Skill council course: Sports, Physical Education, Fitness & Leisure Skills Council (SPEFL-SC) and Beauty & Wellness Sector Skill Council; their qualification packs (QPs) are listed on the National Qualification Register (NQR). Qualifications on the NQR for this work include General Fitness Trainer (NSQF level 4); Community Sports Coach (NSQF level 5); Swimming Pool Lifeguard (NSQF level 4). Check that a training centre is accredited for the exact qualification before you enrol.

Verified fairs tagged to this domain: 10. Typical progression on this page is labelled NEST view; we give no salaries.

Levels in this domain

Roles here are evolving along the AI role ladder. Most people start at L1, Intelligent Proxy: the trained person on site who uses expert-written AI skills and stays accountable for the physical work.

What each rung looks like here

Composite sketches, not real people, grounded in the tools and employers on this page. NEST view

L1 Intelligent Proxy: a day at this rung

Trainer. This person loads a session-planning skill. On the job, this person still watches form and corrects each client. Where they stop and consult: pain, injury or unusual readings go to a doctor or physiotherapist. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L2 Orchestrator: a day at this rung

Gym or academy manager. This person loads agents for batches, bookings and progress notes. On the job, this person still coaches on the floor and checks each plan. Where they stop and consult: medical clearances go to qualified professionals. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L3 Skill developer: a day at this rung

Senior coach. This person loads an injury-safe training-plan skill. On the job, this person still tests it with athletes on the field. Where they stop and consult: medical content is reviewed by a sports-medicine professional. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L4 Harness and model builder: a day at this rung

Sports-tech developer. This person loads movement-analysis and training-load models built with coaches. On the job, this person still films and checks sessions with coaches. Where they stop and consult: results are confirmed by the coach before athletes act on them. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

Skill ladder

EntryGym floor trainee, salon or spa assistant, assistant trek guideTypical next step: Get certified: an ITI trade, apprenticeship or skill-council course (see Start here)
SkilledCertified fitness trainer or yoga instructor, sports coach, beauty or spa therapist, trek leaderTypical next step: Lead a shift or crew and learn to run AI tools for the team (L2)
SeniorHead coach, studio or salon manager, expedition leaderTypical next step: Write and test skills others can load (L3), or train others
Trainer / assessorSSC-certified trainer or assessor (Sports SSC, B&WSSC), academy master trainer, NIS-qualified coach educatorTypical next step: Turn assessments into tested skills, or work with builders on tools (L3 to L4)

Demand signals

  • Beauty & Wellness SSC skill-gap study: workforce of 12.3 million (2022) rising to 26.3 million (2030). Source: B&WSSC skill-gap study
  • The Sports Authority of India had 554 vacant posts, including 90 head-coach posts (Rajya Sabha reply, reported by Sportstar). Source: Sportstar

What we read in this: We read this as trainers, therapists and trek leaders absorbing the growth; reading a person's form and symptoms in person is the bottleneck skill. L1 is the most accessible rung in the next 12 to 18 months. NEST view

How they run their techOfficial blog: newly hired trainers take a certification course of about 30 days at the Cult Training Academy, plus training in fire & life safety, time management and leadership. Its app and data stack is TBC.

Indiahikes

Himalayan trek operator with its own trek leaders

How they run their techOfficial safety page: trek leaders train in basic life support and take at least 4–6 months to qualify; a daily health card lets the leader monitor each trekker's readings instead of relying on intuition.

Source: Indiahikes — How Indiahikes is making your trek safe · checked 11 Oct 2026

What it changesDaily health cards turn trek leaders' judgement into a recorded check; leaders still decide on the mountain, now with written data (L1). NEST view
