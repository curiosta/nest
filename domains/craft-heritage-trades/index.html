Craft & heritage trades

Weaving, pottery, carving and heritage restoration are valued because a skilled person makes them by hand. Design software can suggest a pattern; the loom, the wheel and the lime mortar still need the craftsperson.

An honest note: most craft melas and trade fairs are sales events, where artisans sell to buyers. They are not hiring fairs. NEST lists only verified hiring fairs here, so this domain's calendar filter may show none. Much craft work is self-employed or runs through co-operatives and producer groups.

Start here

Online selling and design tools widen the market; the making itself stays by hand. Most people enter as apprentice weaver, potter's or carver's helper, conservation site helper. The routes below get you to L1; the ladder underneath shows how the work changes at each rung. NEST view

L1 These routes prepare you for L1, Intelligent Proxy. The next rung, L2 Orchestrator, needs time on the job running a crew's work plus several AI tools. NEST view

  • ITI trade (DGT CTS list): Wood Carving Artisan, Hand Embroidery Artisan, Bamboo Works, Carpet Weaving Artisan - Handloom.
  • Apprenticeship: NAPS, through apprenticeshipindia.gov.in (PIB).
  • Job fairs and career centres: National Career Service (NCS).
  • Skill council course: Handicrafts and Carpet Sector Skill Council (HCSSC); their qualification packs (QPs) are listed on the National Qualification Register (NQR). Qualifications on the NQR for this work include Two Shaft Handloom Weaver (NSQF level 3); Carpet Weaver (NSQF level 3.5); Carving Artisan - Stonecraft (NSQF level 3.5). Check that a training centre is accredited for the exact qualification before you enrol.

Roles here are evolving along the AI role ladder. Most people start at L1, Intelligent Proxy: the trained person on site who uses expert-written AI skills and stays accountable for the physical work.

What each rung looks like here

Composite sketches, not real people, grounded in the tools and employers on this page. NEST view

L1 Intelligent Proxy: a day at this rung

Artisan. This person loads a product-listing skill in a phone browser. On the job, this person still makes the piece by hand. Where they stop and consult: pricing for export and legal terms go to the co-op lead. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L2 Orchestrator: a day at this rung

Producer-group coordinator. This person loads agents for orders, costing and dispatch. On the job, this person still checks each piece before dispatch. Where they stop and consult: disputes with buyers go to the producer group's board. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L3 Skill developer: a day at this rung

Master craftsperson. This person loads a quality-check skill for a craft. On the job, this person still makes and inspects pieces to test it. Where they stop and consult: heritage restoration choices go to a conservation expert. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L4 Harness and model builder: a day at this rung

Craft-tech developer. This person loads design-pattern or catalogue models trained on a cluster's own work. On the job, this person still works with artisans to check the outputs. Where they stop and consult: artisans decide what designs are used. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

Trainer / assessorHCSSC certified trainer or assessor, master craftsperson running cluster training, conservation-course instructorTypical next step: Turn assessments into tested skills, or work with builders on tools (L3 to L4)

Demand signals

  • PIB backgrounder (Dec 2025): an estimated 64.66 lakh handloom and handicraft artisans; the Pehchan artisan ID programme has registered over 32 lakh artisans, nearly 20 lakh of them women. Source: PIB (Dec 2025)

What we read in this: We read artisan registration as the craft workforce moving online to sell; product listing and quality checks are the skills that unlock buyers. L1 is the most accessible rung in the next 12 to 18 months. NEST view

How roles and teams are shifting

The craft itself stays by hand; change is upstream, in selling, catalogues and links to buyers. Coordinators who run the digital side for artisans work as L1-L2 proxies. NEST view

TRIFED (Tribes India)

Government body marketing tribal artisans' handicraft, handloom and natural products

How they run their techPIB (Ministry of Tribal Affairs, 17 Dec 2025): around 110 Tribes India outlets (own, consignment and franchise) and a rebuilt e-commerce portal, tribesindia.com.

Source: PIB, 17 Dec 2025 · checked 11 Oct 2026

What it changesSelling moves partly online, so tribal producers and outlet staff need help with listings and orders; a proxy who runs the portal for a producer group is an L1 role. NEST view

Industry trade fairs (not hiring fairs)

These are trade exhibitions where companies show products to buyers. They are not job fairs, so they are not on the calendar. They are still useful for seeing the equipment and the employers in this field. Dates are from the official sites, checked 19 Oct 2026.
