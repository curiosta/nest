🏺 Domain

Craft & heritage trades

Weaving, pottery, carving and heritage restoration are valued because a skilled person makes them by hand. Design software can suggest a pattern; the loom, the wheel and the lime mortar still need the craftsperson.

An honest note: most craft melas and trade fairs are sales events, where artisans sell to buyers. They are not hiring fairs. NEST lists only verified hiring fairs here, so this domain's calendar filter may show none. Much craft work is self-employed or runs through co-operatives and producer groups.

Demand signals

  • PIB backgrounder (Dec 2025): an estimated 64.66 lakh handloom and handicraft artisans; the Pehchan artisan ID programme has registered over 32 lakh artisans, nearly 20 lakh of them women. Source: PIB (Dec 2025)
