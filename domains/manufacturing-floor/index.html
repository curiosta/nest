Manufacturing floor & maintenance

Machine setting, maintenance, and quality on the shop floor stay human-heavy until robots take over dull/dangerous stations. Agents can schedule; they cannot yet feel a vibration fault.

Electronics, chip assembly and test (OSAT) and robot assembly and maintenance now have their own domain: Electronics, chips & robotics.

Start here

More robots and vision systems on the line mean more work for the people who set them up and repair them. Most people enter as operator trainee, assembler, helper. The routes below get you to L1; the ladder underneath shows how the work changes at each rung. NEST view

L1 These routes prepare you for L1, Intelligent Proxy. The next rung, L2 Orchestrator, needs time on the job running a crew's work plus several AI tools. NEST view

  • ITI trade (DGT CTS list): Fitter, Turner, Machinist, CNC MACHINING TECHNICIAN.
  • Apprenticeship: NAPS, through apprenticeshipindia.gov.in (PIB).
  • Job fairs and career centres: National Career Service (NCS).
  • Skill council course: Capital Goods Skill Council (CGSC) and Automotive Skills Development Council (ASDC); their qualification packs (QPs) are listed on the National Qualification Register (NQR). Qualifications on the NQR for this work include CNC Operator - Vertical Machining Centre (NSQF level 4); Assistant Electrician (NSQF level 3). Check that a training centre is accredited for the exact qualification before you enrol.

Levels in this domain

Roles here are evolving along the AI role ladder. Most people start at L1, Intelligent Proxy: the trained person on site who uses expert-written AI skills and stays accountable for the physical work.

What each rung looks like here

Composite sketches, not real people, grounded in the tools and employers on this page. NEST view

L1 Intelligent Proxy: a day at this rung

Maintenance technician. This person loads a fault-finding skill at the machine. On the job, this person still isolates the machine, opens the panel and replaces the part. Where they stop and consult: high-voltage or safety-interlock faults go to the maintenance engineer. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L2 Orchestrator: a day at this rung

Shift lead. This person loads agents for downtime logs and spares orders. On the job, this person still walks the line, confirms each logged stop and talks to operators. Where they stop and consult: repeated quality failures go to the quality head. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L3 Skill developer: a day at this rung

Senior maintenance engineer. This person loads a changeover skill tested on the shop floor. On the job, this person still runs the changeover with operators to time each step. Where they stop and consult: changes to safety procedures are approved by the plant safety officer. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L4 Harness and model builder: a day at this rung

Manufacturing-AI engineer. This person loads visual-inspection and predictive-maintenance models trained on line data. On the job, this person still stands at the line with technicians to check false alarms. Where they stop and consult: a model is released only after quality and maintenance teams sign off. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

Demand signals

  • India installed a record 9,120 industrial robots in 2024, sixth in the world; each line needs operators and maintainers. Source: International Federation of Robotics (25 Sep 2025)

What we read in this: We read this as robot and line growth flowing to maintenance electricians, mechatronics technicians and setters; fault-finding on automated lines is the bottleneck skill. L1 is the most accessible rung in the next 12 to 18 months, with L2 open to shift leads. NEST view

How roles and teams are shifting

Vision AI, IIoT and digital twins let fewer supervisors oversee more of the line. Operators run automated cells, maintainers become more skilled, and engineers who tune systems move up. NEST view

Human in the loop here

As AI cameras inspect more parts, people check the flagged ones and teach the system what it got wrong. NEST view These are Human in the Loop roles: people who supervise, correct, approve or take over from AI and robots. All HITL roles and how they map to L1–L4 →

  • On-site AI quality inspector: Checks parts that an AI camera flags, decides pass or fail, and corrects the system when it is wrong. NEST view
    Evidence: Maruti Suzuki press release: startup programme that includes AI visual inspection; Jidoka case study: Maruti Suzuki engine-bore inspection with a Human-in-the-Loop framework (vendor's own account) To confirm
  • Robot teleoperation: Steers a robot through a task by remote control or VR, takes over when it gets stuck, and records demonstrations it can learn from. NEST view
    Evidence: NEST's recording ladder: workers recorded as data for robots, with Indian examples
  • AI output review and evaluation: Rates and corrects AI answers, images or robot clips against a rubric, so the next version improves. Knowing the trade, such as nursing or wiring, makes the review better. NEST view
    Evidence: CNBC (Jun 2026): Indian firms that recruit people to record task videos for robots also check quality parameters and annotate data; MIT Technology Review (1 Apr 2026): Micro1 task videos are reviewed by AI and a human

Also: Maruti Suzuki press release: startup programme that includes AI visual inspection.
