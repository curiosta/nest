Electronics, chips & robotics

Phones, chips and robots are built on factory lines and in clean rooms by operators and technicians who place, solder, test and repair. Software can design and inspect; people still load the machines, assemble, and fix what breaks.

Split out on 12 Oct 2026 from Manufacturing floor & maintenance, which keeps automotive, process and general factory work. Drone flying stays under Survey & geospatial field.

Demand signals

  • 12 semiconductor projects approved in six states, including 9 assembly and test (ATMP/OSAT) units; commercial production has started at Micron, Kaynes Semicon and CG Power OSAT. Electronics manufacturing created about 25 lakh jobs over the last decade; women are about 30% of its workforce. Source: PIB (15 Jul 2026): India Semiconductor Mission, 12 projects in six states
  • CG Semi's two Sanand facilities are projected to create more than 5,000 direct and indirect jobs; it sent Indian engineers, operators and technicians to Malaysia for three months of hands-on training. Source: PIB (28 Aug 2025): CG Semi OSAT pilot line, Sanand
  • Tata Electronics' Assam assembly and test facility is expected to create more than 27,000 direct and indirect jobs. Source: Tata Electronics (29 Feb 2024): Assam semiconductor assembly and test facility
  • India installed a record 9,120 industrial robots in 2024, sixth in the world. Source: International Federation of Robotics (25 Sep 2025): India sixth in robot installations
