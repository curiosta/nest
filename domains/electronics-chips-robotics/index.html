Electronics, chips & robotics

Phones, chips and robots are built on factory lines and in clean rooms by operators and technicians who place, solder, test and repair. Software can design and inspect; people still load the machines, assemble, and fix what breaks.

Split out on 12 Oct 2026 from Manufacturing floor & maintenance, which keeps automotive, process and general factory work. Drone flying stays under Survey & geospatial field.

Start here

AI runs on chips and robots that people assemble, test and keep running. Most people enter as line operator, assembler or cleanroom operator trainee. The routes below get you to L1; the ladder underneath shows how the work changes at each rung. NEST view

L1 These routes prepare you for L1, Intelligent Proxy. The next rung, L2 Orchestrator, needs time on the job running a crew's work plus several AI tools. NEST view

  • ITI trade (DGT CTS list): Electronics Mechanic, Semiconductor Technician, Technician Mechatronics, Industrial Robotics & Digital Manufacturing Technician.
  • Apprenticeship: NAPS, through apprenticeshipindia.gov.in (PIB).
  • Job fairs and career centres: National Career Service (NCS).
  • Skill council course: Electronics Sector Skills Council of India (ESSCI); their qualification packs (QPs) are listed on the National Qualification Register (NQR). Qualifications on the NQR for this work include Clean-Room Operations (for semiconductors) (NSQF level 4); Electronic Hardware Assembly Operator (NSQF level 4); Assistant Robotics Technician (Introduction) (NSQF level 3). Check that a training centre is accredited for the exact qualification before you enrol.

Verified fairs tagged to this domain: 8. Typical progression on this page is labelled NEST view; we give no salaries.

Levels in this domain

  1. L1Intelligent ProxyUses expert AI skills; does the physical work
  2. L2OrchestratorRuns several AI tools and a crew's workflow
  3. L3Skill developerWrites the skills others use
  4. L4Harness and model builderBuilds the tools skills run on

Roles here are evolving along the AI role ladder. Most people start at L1, Intelligent Proxy: the trained person on site who uses expert-written AI skills and stays accountable for the physical work.

Example roles at each rung. NEST view

  1. L1 Intelligent ProxyLine operator using a setup and defect-check skill Example: CG Semi's pilot line runs on a Manufacturing Execution System for automation and traceability
  2. L2 OrchestratorLine supervisor running agents for yield reports and maintenance tickets Example: Addverb's mobile robots, forklifts and sorters run on its own software
  3. L3 Skill developerProcess engineer writing a robot-recovery skill NEST view
  4. L4 Harness and model builderRobotics engineer building robot controllers and fleet software for warehouses and production lines. Example: Addverb's robots run on its own software

What each rung looks like here

Composite sketches, not real people, grounded in the tools and employers on this page. NEST view

L1 Intelligent Proxy: a day at this rung

Line operator. This person loads a setup and defect-check skill. On the job, this person still loads and sets up the machine and clears jams. Where they stop and consult: repeated defects go to the process engineer. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L2 Orchestrator: a day at this rung

Line supervisor. This person loads agents for yield reports and maintenance tickets. On the job, this person still walks the line and checks each report. Where they stop and consult: safety incidents go to the EHS team. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L3 Skill developer: a day at this rung

Process engineer. This person loads a robot-recovery skill. On the job, this person still recovers real robots to test it. Where they stop and consult: safety changes are approved by the safety engineer. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L4 Harness and model builder: a day at this rung

Robotics engineer. This person loads robot controllers and fleet software. On the job, this person still tests robots on the floor. Where they stop and consult: robots run near people only after safety validation. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

SkilledSMT or hand-soldering technician, test or equipment technician, robot and cobot maintainerTypical next step: Lead a shift or crew and learn to run AI tools for the team (L2)
SeniorLine or shift supervisor, process or equipment engineerTypical next step: Write and test skills others can load (L3), or train others

Demand signals

  • 12 semiconductor projects approved in six states, including 9 assembly and test (ATMP/OSAT) units; commercial production has started at Micron, Kaynes Semicon and CG Power OSAT. Electronics manufacturing created about 25 lakh jobs over the last decade; women are about 30% of its workforce. Source: PIB (15 Jul 2026): India Semiconductor Mission, 12 projects in six states
  • CG Semi's two Sanand facilities are projected to create more than 5,000 direct and indirect jobs; it sent Indian engineers, operators and technicians to Malaysia for three months of hands-on training. Source: PIB (28 Aug 2025): CG Semi OSAT pilot line, Sanand
  • Tata Electronics' Assam assembly and test facility is expected to create more than 27,000 direct and indirect jobs. Source: Tata Electronics (29 Feb 2024): Assam semiconductor assembly and test facility
  • India installed a record 9,120 industrial robots in 2024, sixth in the world. Source: International Federation of Robotics (25 Sep 2025): India sixth in robot installations

What we read in this: We read new assembly and test units as demand for line operators, SMT and equipment technicians; cleanroom discipline and robot maintenance are the bottleneck skills. L1 is the most accessible rung in the next 12 to 18 months. NEST view

How roles and teams are shifting

Production systems with traceability and warehouse robots mean operators follow system-driven steps and supervise fleets. Process and failure-analysis engineers who write recipes move up. NEST view

Human in the loop here

More robots means more people steering, correcting and labelling for them. NEST view These are Human in the Loop roles: people who supervise, correct, approve or take over from AI and robots. All HITL roles and how they map to L1–L4 →

  • Robot teleoperation: Steers a robot through a task by remote control or VR, takes over when it gets stuck, and records demonstrations it can learn from. NEST view
    Evidence: NEST's recording ladder: workers recorded as data for robots, with Indian examples
  • On-site AI quality inspector: Checks parts that an AI camera flags, decides pass or fail, and corrects the system when it is wrong. NEST view
    Evidence: Maruti Suzuki press release: startup programme that includes AI visual inspection; Jidoka case study: Maruti Suzuki engine-bore inspection with a Human-in-the-Loop framework (vendor's own account) To confirm
  • Data labelling and annotation: Labels images, video, text, speech or sensor data so models can learn, and corrects labels the model got wrong. NEST view
    Evidence: Lok Sabha answer (22 Jul 2026): 27 IndiaAI Data and AI Labs at NIELIT centres are operational; another 188 at ITIs and polytechnics are underway; PIB: IndiaAI courses for Data Annotator and Data Curator roles, taught in Data Labs in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities
