Animal care & veterinary

Helping a cow calve, vaccinating a flock, treating an injured dog or rescuing a leopard from a well needs hands, calm and judgement. Herd apps and helplines route the call; a trained person examines the animal.

Works hand in hand with Agri / food processing / cold chain: dairy, poultry and fisheries feed that domain's supply chains.

Demand signals

  • Lok Sabha answer (Jul 2026): 4,019 Mobile Veterinary Units operational in 29 States / UTs; farmers call the toll-free 1962 line and call centres with veterinarians dispatch a unit. Source: Lok Sabha (DAHD)
  • NDDB Annual Report 2024-25: 1,683 Multi-Purpose AI Technicians (MAITRIs) deployed across the North-Eastern states performed over 46.62 lakh artificial inseminations. Source: NDDB

NDDB (National Dairy Development Board)

Dairy development body supporting co-operatives' veterinary and breeding services

How they run their techAnnual Report 2024-25: monitored and supervised the artificial-insemination network in the North-East, where 1,683 MAITRIs performed over 46.62 lakh inseminations; co-operatives deliver veterinary care, AI, vaccination and feed services to farmers.

Source: NDDB Annual Report 2024-25 · checked 11 Oct 2026

What it changesInsemination technicians (MAITRIs) stay hands-on; coordination moves to the co-operative and NDDB level, where data on services decides where field effort goes (L2). NEST view

Source: Suguna Foods — infrastructure · checked 11 Oct 2026

How roles and teams are shifting

Records, call centres and ERP link field vets and farm supervisors to central teams. Hands-on animal care stays human; coordination and review move up the ladder. NEST view

Levels in this domain

  1. L1Intelligent ProxyUses expert AI skills; does the physical work
  2. L2OrchestratorRuns several AI tools and a crew's workflow
  3. L3Skill developerWrites the skills others use
  4. L4Harness and model builderBuilds the tools skills run on

Roles here are evolving along the AI role ladder. Most people start at L1, Intelligent Proxy: the trained person on site who uses expert-written AI skills and stays accountable for the physical work.

With AI / For AI

With AI

Herd records, ear-tag data and helpline triage plan the visit (NDDB and Suguna; see leaders above). Examining, injecting and handling the animal stays hands-on. NEST view

For AI

Few jobs here exist because of AI. Sensor collars and herd-data apps need field staff who fit them and act on alerts. NEST view
