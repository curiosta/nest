Agri / food processing / cold chain

Primary production and wet processing happen in physical plants and fields. Agents may advise agronomy; harvesting, processing lines, and cold-store work stay embodied.

Livestock, dairy, poultry and veterinary work has its own domain since 11 Oct 2026: Animal care & veterinary.

Start here

Voice advisers and drones reach farmers, but someone trained still has to fly, spray, process and check the food. Most people enter as farm worker with machinery training, plant helper. The routes below get you to L1; the ladder underneath shows how the work changes at each rung. NEST view

L1 These routes prepare you for L1, Intelligent Proxy. The next rung, L2 Orchestrator, needs time on the job running a crew's work plus several AI tools. NEST view

  • ITI trade (DGT CTS list): Horticulture, Agro Processing, Food Production (General), Dairying.
  • Apprenticeship: NAPS, through apprenticeshipindia.gov.in (PIB).
  • Job fairs and career centres: National Career Service (NCS).
  • Skill council course: Agriculture Skill Council of India (ASCI) and Food Industry Council for Skills and Innovation (FICSI); their qualification packs (QPs) are listed on the National Qualification Register (NQR). Qualifications on the NQR for this work include Micro-Irrigation Technician (NSQF level 4); Assistant Technician – Cold Storage (NSQF level 3); Kisan Drone Operator (NSQF level 3). Check that a training centre is accredited for the exact qualification before you enrol.

Verified fairs tagged to this domain: 9. Typical progression on this page is labelled NEST view; we give no salaries.

Levels in this domain

  1. L1Intelligent ProxyUses expert AI skills; does the physical work
  2. L2OrchestratorRuns several AI tools and a crew's workflow
  3. L3Skill developerWrites the skills others use
  4. L4Harness and model builderBuilds the tools skills run on

Roles here are evolving along the AI role ladder. Most people start at L1, Intelligent Proxy: the trained person on site who uses expert-written AI skills and stays accountable for the physical work.

Example roles at each rung. NEST view

  1. L1 Intelligent ProxyFarmer using a scheme-application skill in a phone browser Example: farmers ask Kisan e-Mitra, a voice AI chatbot, in their own language (PIB)
  2. L2 OrchestratorFPO manager running agents for procurement, inputs and buyer follow-up Example: Amul's call centre, Pashu Seva app, ear-tag records and service ticketing run as one system
  3. L3 Skill developerAgronomist writing a pest-scouting and spray-safety skill NEST view
  4. L4 Harness and model builderAgri-tech engineer with agronomists, building and fine-tuning crop-advisory and crop-disease models for local crops and languages. Example: ITC's Krishi Mitra GenAI co-pilot (ITCMAARS)

What each rung looks like here

Composite sketches, not real people, grounded in the tools and employers on this page. NEST view

L1 Intelligent Proxy: a day at this rung

Farmer. This person loads a scheme-application skill and a voice farm adviser on the phone. On the job, this person still works the field, sprays and harvests. Where they stop and consult: pesticide doses and crop disease beyond the basics go to the agriculture officer or a Krishi Vigyan Kendra expert. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L2 Orchestrator: a day at this rung

FPO manager. This person loads agents for procurement, inputs and buyer follow-up. On the job, this person still visits members' fields and checks produce at collection. Where they stop and consult: contract and payment disputes go to the FPO board. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L3 Skill developer: a day at this rung

Agronomist. This person loads a pest-scouting and spray-safety skill. On the job, this person still scouts real fields with farmers to test it. Where they stop and consult: chemical advice is checked against official guidance before release. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L4 Harness and model builder: a day at this rung

Agri-tech engineer. This person loads crop-advisory and crop-disease models for local crops and languages. On the job, this person still visits plots to collect and check ground truth. Where they stop and consult: advice is validated with agronomists before farmers see it. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

How roles and teams are shifting

How leaders run their tech, and what it changes

ITC (Agri Business / ITCMAARS)

Large agri sourcing & foods player engaging farm + processing value chains

How they run their techOfficial ITCMAARS “phygital” ecosystem: farmer super-app, soil tests, remote sensing, IoT weather/moisture sensors, drone fertilisation services, market-price intelligence, and vernacular GenAI co-pilot (Krishi Mitra) — digital advisory layered on FPO field networks.

Source: ITC — ITCMAARS NextGen Agriculture · checked 6 Oct 2026

What it changesField extension staff shift from visiting every farm to acting on app, sensor and satellite alerts; farmer-facing staff work as L1 proxies with the co-pilot, while agronomists move up to writing the advisories (L3). NEST view

Amul Dairy (Kaira Union / GCMMF network)

Cooperative dairy system employing plant, cold-chain & field vet/collection roles

How they run their techOfficial digitalisation page: 24×7 Centralized Veterinary Call Center, Amul Pashu Seva app, animal ear-tagging integrated with treatment/AI software (~99% tagged in milk-shed), BMC/plant service ticketing, GPS vehicle tracking for vet vans, and SMS case workflows.

Source: Amul Dairy — Digitalization of Veterinary Services · checked 6 Oct 2026

What it changesVillage paravets and call-centre staff work from tagged-animal records and tickets, so dispatch and follow-up merge into one digital loop; supervisors orchestrate the queue (L2). NEST view

Godrej Agrovet

Agribusiness employer across animal feed, oil palm, crop protection and processing

How they run their techGodrej Agrovet's chairman's letter for 2024-25 describes a strategic digital transformation journey: a Farmer Management System app and digital integration across the supply chain to improve agility and traceability, plus real-time communication platforms and AI-driven advisory services for farmers.

Human in the loop here

Farm drones need trained pilots on the ground: spraying and mapping are flown and watched by a person. NEST view These are Human in the Loop roles: people who supervise, correct, approve or take over from AI and robots. All HITL roles and how they map to L1–L4 →

  • Drone pilot: Flies survey, farm-spraying, inspection or show drones, watches the autopilot and takes manual control when needed. NEST view
    Evidence: PIB, Drone Rules 2021: DGCA oversees drone schools; the remote pilot licence follows a remote pilot certificate from an authorised drone school; PIB: setting up a Remote Pilot Training Organisation (RPTO) needs DGCA authorisation through the Digital Sky platform

Also: NQR qualification: Kisan Drone Operator; PIB: Namo Drone Didi scheme.
