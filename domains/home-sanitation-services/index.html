Home, sanitation & waste services

Fixing a leak, cooking for a family, driving, cleaning a sewer safely, sorting recyclables or caring for the dead all happen in homes and streets. Apps can book the job; a trained person does it.

App-based work (for example on Urban Company) is gig or platform work: check how pay, insurance and ID verification work before you join. Appliance and AC repair sits under Repair & field maintenance.

Start here

Apps route the jobs; the pipe, the meal and the safe sewer entry are still done by hand. Most people enter as helper, housekeeper, waste-collection crew, cook's assistant. The routes below get you to L1; the ladder underneath shows how the work changes at each rung. NEST view

L1 These routes prepare you for L1, Intelligent Proxy. The next rung, L2 Orchestrator, needs time on the job running a crew's work plus several AI tools. NEST view

  • ITI trade (DGT CTS list): Plumber, Health Sanitary Inspector, Housekeeper.
  • Apprenticeship: NAPS, through apprenticeshipindia.gov.in (PIB).
  • Job fairs and career centres: National Career Service (NCS).
  • Skill council course: Indian Plumbing Skills Council (IPSC); their qualification packs (QPs) are listed on the National Qualification Register (NQR). Qualifications on the NQR for this work include Plumber - General (NSQF level 4); Assistant Plumber - General (NSQF level 3); Industrial Wastewater Treatment Plant Operator (NSQF level 4). Check that a training centre is accredited for the exact qualification before you enrol.

Verified fairs tagged to this domain: 7. Typical progression on this page is labelled NEST view; we give no salaries.

Levels in this domain

Roles here are evolving along the AI role ladder. Most people start at L1, Intelligent Proxy: the trained person on site who uses expert-written AI skills and stays accountable for the physical work.

What each rung looks like here

Composite sketches, not real people, grounded in the tools and employers on this page. NEST view

L1 Intelligent Proxy: a day at this rung

Plumber. This person loads a leak-diagnosis skill on a phone. On the job, this person still opens the pipe and fixes the leak. Where they stop and consult: gas leaks and electrical faults go to licensed specialists. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L2 Orchestrator: a day at this rung

Service-business owner. This person loads agents for bookings, quotes and follow-ups. On the job, this person still visits jobs and checks the crew's work. Where they stop and consult: safety incidents go to the authorities. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L3 Skill developer: a day at this rung

Sanitation expert. This person loads a safe confined-space entry skill. On the job, this person still trains crews with full safety gear. Where they stop and consult: nobody enters a sewer without the safety equipment and supervision the rules require. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L4 Harness and model builder: a day at this rung

Ops-AI developer. This person loads route-planning and job-matching models. On the job, this person still rides with crews to see the routes. Where they stop and consult: changes to safety rules need the operator's safety head. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

SeniorHousekeeping or waste-collection zone supervisor, partner-team lead on an app platformTypical next step: Write and test skills others can load (L3), or train others
Trainer / assessorPlatform or agency trainer, SSC-certified trainer or assessor (plumbing, domestic work), sanitation-safety trainerTypical next step: Turn assessments into tested skills, or work with builders on tools (L3 to L4)

Demand signals

  • NITI Aayog: India's gig workforce of 77 lakh (2020-21) is expected to reach 2.35 crore by 2029-30. Source: PIB (NITI Aayog report, Jun 2022)
  • NAMASTE scheme, as of 20 Sep 2025: 88,448 sewer and septic-tank workers and 42,127 waste pickers validated; workers receive occupational-safety training and PPE kits. Source: PIB (Sep 2025)

What we read in this: We read gig-platform growth as demand for certified plumbers, cleaners and cooks, and safety training for sanitation workers as the bottleneck. L1 is the most accessible rung in the next 12 to 18 months. NEST view

Urban Company

Home-services platform for cleaning, plumbing, carpentry, beauty and repairs

How they run their techAnnual Report 2024-25: in-house training plus tools and consumables for service professionals; InstaHelp, a pilot launched in Mumbai in January 2025, books house help for cleaning and mopping within 30 minutes.

Source: Urban Company Annual Report 2024-25 · checked 11 Oct 2026

What it changesIn home services, the platform supplies training, tools and booking; professionals do the physical job and the app handles scheduling and payment, so the platform replaces the old middleman. NEST view

Antony Waste Handling Cell

Municipal solid-waste collection and processing contractor

How they run their techQ4 FY25 investor presentation: GPS tracking devices on 2,212 of its 2,514 vehicles to track movement, optimise routes and raise vehicle utilisation.

Source: Antony Waste Q4 & FY25 investor presentation · checked 11 Oct 2026

What it changesGPS on most vehicles moves route planning and supervision to a control room; drivers and crews follow optimised routes, and supervisors orchestrate the fleet (L2). NEST view

Updater Services (UDS)

Source: UDS Annual Report 2023-24 · checked 11 Oct 2026

Human in the loop here

Robots now go down the manhole, but a trained sanitation worker drives them from the surface. NEST view These are Human in the Loop roles: people who supervise, correct, approve or take over from AI and robots. All HITL roles and how they map to L1–L4 →

  • Sewer-cleaning robot operator: Steers a manhole-cleaning robot from the surface using its cameras and controls, and decides when the job is done safely. NEST view
    Evidence: UT of Ladakh: Leh's municipal committee launched Genrobotics' Bandicoot robot and directed that sanitation workers be trained to operate it; Indian Express (Pune): a former manhole-cleaning labourer trained to use the Bandicoot robot says he is now its operator; PIB (9 Aug 2023): under Section 33 of the MS Act, 2013, local authorities are supposed to use appropriate technological appliances to clean sewers and septic tanks; the NAMASTE scheme aims for all sanitation work to be done by skilled workers
    Routes in: Operator training through the municipal body or the machine's maker. To confirm for your city.
