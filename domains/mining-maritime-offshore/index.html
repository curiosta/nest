Mining, maritime & offshore

Mines, ships, ports, shipyards and offshore rigs are hazardous places where licensed people keep watch, blast, weld and sail. Drones and control rooms help; the certificate holder on site is accountable.

Seafarer jobs are filled through DG Shipping-licensed recruitment and placement (RPS) agencies, not open job fairs. Check an agency's licence before paying any fee.

Radar, drones and digital tools flag the risk; licensed people on site still decide and act. Most people enter as mining helper or trainee, GP rating, shipyard apprentice. The routes below get you to L1; the ladder underneath shows how the work changes at each rung. NEST view

  • ITI trade (DGT CTS list): Stone Mining Machine Operator, Mechanic Mining Machinery, Marine Fitter.
  • Apprenticeship: NAPS, through apprenticeshipindia.gov.in (PIB).
  • Job fairs and career centres: National Career Service (NCS).
  • Skill council course: Skill Council for Mining Sector; their qualification packs (QPs) are listed on the National Qualification Register (NQR). Specific QP names: To confirm.

Levels in this domain

  1. L1Intelligent ProxyUses expert AI skills; does the physical work
  2. L2OrchestratorRuns several AI tools and a crew's workflow
  3. L3Skill developerWrites the skills others use
  4. L4Harness and model builderBuilds the tools skills run on

Roles here are evolving along the AI role ladder. Most people start at L1, Intelligent Proxy: the trained person on site who uses expert-written AI skills and stays accountable for the physical work.

Example roles at each rung. NEST view

  1. L1 Intelligent ProxyShift operator using a pre-start safety skill Example: Coal India subsidiaries survey with drones (Ministry of Coal)
  2. L2 OrchestratorShift supervisor running agents for logs, permits and handovers Example: NMDC's fleet management and automatic sampling
  3. L3 Skill developerSenior engineer writing a hazard-reporting skill Example: SCI's Maritime Training Institute runs pre-sea and STCW courses
  4. L4 Harness and model builderMining or marine-tech engineer building slope-risk, ore-grade or voyage models from drone, radar and sensor data. NEST view

What each rung looks like here

Composite sketches, not real people, grounded in the tools and employers on this page. NEST view

L1 Intelligent Proxy: a day at this rung

Shift operator. This person loads a pre-start safety skill. On the job, this person still inspects the equipment and works the shift. Where they stop and consult: unsafe ground or machine faults go to the overman or the ship's officer. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L2 Orchestrator: a day at this rung

Shift supervisor. This person loads agents for logs, permits and handovers. On the job, this person still checks each work area before signing permits. Where they stop and consult: statutory matters go to the mine manager or master. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L3 Skill developer: a day at this rung

Senior engineer. This person loads a hazard-reporting skill. On the job, this person still walks the site with crews to test it. Where they stop and consult: the skill follows DGMS or maritime rules as checked by the safety officer. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

L4 Harness and model builder: a day at this rung

Mining or marine-tech engineer. This person loads slope-risk, ore-grade or voyage models. On the job, this person still goes on site to check sensor and drone data. Where they stop and consult: decisions stay with licensed managers and officers. The AI speeds up the routine part; the judgement and the accountability stay with the person. NEST view

Skill ladder

EntryMining helper or trainee, GP rating, shipyard apprenticeTypical next step: Get certified: an ITI trade, apprenticeship or skill-council course (see Start here)
SkilledMining mate or blaster (DGMS certificate), able seafarer or motorman, certified welder or fitterTypical next step: Lead a shift or crew and learn to run AI tools for the team (L2)
SeniorOverman or foreman, ship's officer or engineer, yard supervisorTypical next step: Write and test skills others can load (L3), or train others
Trainer / assessorMining SSC certified trainer or assessor, DG Shipping-approved maritime institute faculty, ITI instructor (CITS)Typical next step: Turn assessments into tested skills, or work with builders on tools (L3 to L4)

Demand signals

  • Lok Sabha answer: 3,23,429 active Indian seafarers (2025), about 12% of the global seafaring workforce; Maritime India Vision 2030 targets a 20% share. Source: Lok Sabha (MoPSW)
  • Skill Council for Mining Sector study: mining-sector employment of about 11.7 million (core, ancillary and induced; 2018-19). Source: SCMS

What we read in this: We read this as certified mining mates, welders and seafarers absorbing the work; statutory certificates are the bottleneck. L1 is the most accessible rung in the next 12 to 18 months, once certified. NEST view

How roles and teams are shifting

Drones, radar, fleet management and automatic sampling take over parts of surveying and dispatch. Certificate holders stay accountable on site; surveyors and supervisors move to checking data (L1-L2). NEST view

Employer notes: how leaders run their tech, and what it changes

How leaders run their tech, and what it changes

How listed employers run ops tech, automation, safety systems and workforce skilling in this physical domain, and what that changes for roles and teams. Notes prefer official strategy / annual-report / IR / careers pages. TBC means not verified this pass — we never invent. Each “What it changes” line is our reading of the cited note (NEST view), placed on the AI role ladder.

Coal India

India's largest coal producer, with mine crews across its subsidiaries

How they run their techMinistry of Coal Annual Report 2024-25: drone-based surface surveys at Coal India subsidiaries; CMPDI's survey-grade drones carry LiDAR, optical and thermal sensors, plus a fixed-wing VTOL drone; a slope-stability radar deployed at Dudhichua opencast mine (NCL).

Source: Ministry of Coal Annual Report 2024-25, chapter 3 · checked 11 Oct 2026

What it changesDrones and slope-stability radar take over parts of manual surveying and watching slopes; surveyors become drone operators and data checkers (L1-L2). NEST view

Cochin Shipyard

Shipbuilding and ship-repair yard with a large trades workforce

How they run their techAnnual Report 2024-25: digitalised operations powered by SAP S/4HANA, 3DEXPERIENCE and an in-house Digital Centre of Excellence; 200+ training programmes and 4,650+ training man-days in the year.

Source: Cochin Shipyard Annual Report 2024-25 · checked 11 Oct 2026

What it changesDigital design and ERP link the drawing office to the yard; tradespeople keep welding and fitting, while planners orchestrate digital workflows and heavy training grows. NEST view

Shipping Corporation of India

State-owned shipping line employing officers and ratings

How they run their techOfficial Maritime Training Institute page: pre-sea courses (including GP rating) and post-sea, STCW and value-added courses for SCI and other Indian shipping companies. Its fleet-technology stack is TBC.

Source: SCI Maritime Training Institute — overview · checked 11 Oct 2026

What it changesIts institute trains ratings and officers for certified roles; technology does not replace the certificate holder on board. NEST view

NMDC

Iron-ore miner with large opencast mines

How they run their techNMDC annual report: IT and digital initiatives including automatic sampling and fleet management at its mines.

Source: NMDC Annual Report 2023-24 · checked 12 Oct 2026

What it changesAutomatic sampling and fleet management move sampling and dispatch from manual work to systems; mine staff supervise machines and act on data (L2). NEST view
