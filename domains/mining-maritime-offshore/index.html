Mining, maritime & offshore

Mines, ships, ports, shipyards and offshore rigs are hazardous places where licensed people keep watch, blast, weld and sail. Drones and control rooms help; the certificate holder on site is accountable.

Seafarer jobs are filled through DG Shipping-licensed recruitment and placement (RPS) agencies, not open job fairs. Check an agency's licence before paying any fee.

Skill ladder

EntryMining helper or trainee, GP rating, shipyard apprentice
SkilledMining mate or blaster (DGMS certificate), able seafarer or motorman, certified welder or fitter
SeniorOverman or foreman, ship's officer or engineer, yard supervisor
Trainer / assessorMining SSC certified trainer or assessor, DG Shipping-approved maritime institute faculty, ITI instructor (CITS)

Demand signals

  • Lok Sabha answer: 3,23,429 active Indian seafarers (2025), about 12% of the global seafaring workforce; Maritime India Vision 2030 targets a 20% share. Source: Lok Sabha (MoPSW)
  • Skill Council for Mining Sector study: mining-sector employment of about 11.7 million (core, ancillary and induced; 2018-19). Source: SCMS

Industry leaders · tech management

How listed employers currently run ops tech, automation, safety systems, and workforce skilling for this physical domain. Notes prefer official strategy / annual-report / IR / careers pages. TBC means not verified this pass — we never invent.

Coal India

India's largest coal producer, with mine crews across its subsidiaries

Current technology managementMinistry of Coal Annual Report 2024-25: drone-based surface surveys at Coal India subsidiaries; CMPDI's survey-grade drones carry LiDAR, optical and thermal sensors, plus a fixed-wing VTOL drone; a slope-stability radar deployed at Dudhichua opencast mine (NCL).

Source: Ministry of Coal Annual Report 2024-25, chapter 3 · checked 11 Oct 2026

Cochin Shipyard

Shipbuilding and ship-repair yard with a large trades workforce

Current technology managementAnnual Report 2024-25: digitalised operations powered by SAP S/4HANA, 3DEXPERIENCE and an in-house Digital Centre of Excellence; 200+ training programmes and 4,650+ training man-days in the year.

Source: Cochin Shipyard Annual Report 2024-25 · checked 11 Oct 2026

Shipping Corporation of India

State-owned shipping line employing officers and ratings

Current technology managementOfficial Maritime Training Institute page: pre-sea courses (including GP rating) and post-sea, STCW and value-added courses for SCI and other Indian shipping companies. Its fleet-technology stack is TBC.

Source: SCI Maritime Training Institute — overview · checked 11 Oct 2026

NMDC

Iron-ore miner with large opencast mines

Current technology managementNMDC annual report: IT and digital initiatives including automatic sampling and fleet management at its mines.

Source: NMDC Annual Report 2023-24 · checked 12 Oct 2026

Levels in this domain

Most people start as an Intelligent Proxy (L1): the trained person on site who uses AI skills and stays accountable for the physical work.

Example roles at each expertise level. NEST view

  1. L1 Intelligent ProxyShift operator using a pre-start safety skill
  2. L2 OrchestratorShift supervisor running agents for logs, permits and handovers
  3. L3 Skill developerSenior engineer writing a hazard-reporting skill
  4. L4 Harness and model builderUsually outside this domain; with domain experts, building evals that test agents on this work
