⚡ Domain

Energy & utilities field

Solar rooftops, wind turbines, power lines and village water schemes are built and kept running by people on roofs, poles and in pump houses. Monitoring centres and AI flag the fault; a trained person still isolates the line and fixes it.

Skill ladder

EntrySolar helper, lineman helper, pump-operator trainee
SkilledSolar PV technician, distribution lineman, wind O&M technician
SeniorSite or substation in-charge, O&M supervisor
Trainer / assessorSSC-certified trainer (ToT) or assessor (ToA) for green-jobs / power QPs; ITI instructor (CITS)

Demand signals

  • CEEW–NRDC (Jun 2026): India's 500 GW and green-hydrogen goals could generate over 44 lakh FTE jobs, about 43% from rooftop solar. Source: CEEW press release
  • Jal Jeevan Mission's Nal Jal Mitra programme trains a multi-skilled village operator per gram panchayat (510-hour NSQF Level 4 course). Source: PIB

Industry leaders · tech management

How listed employers currently run ops tech, automation, safety systems, and workforce skilling for this physical domain. Notes prefer official strategy / annual-report / IR / careers pages. TBC means not verified this pass — we never invent.

Tata Power

Integrated utility (generation, transmission, distribution, rooftop solar) with large field crews

Current technology managementIntegrated Annual Report 2024-25: 26+ lakh smart meters installed across its DISCOMs; drone surveillance with AI-driven feeder analytics; AI-powered robotic condition monitoring for remote stations; AI-powered predictive maintenance and grid optimisation; robotic panel cleaning in its technology roadmap.

Source: Tata Power Integrated Annual Report 2024-25 · checked 11 Oct 2026

NTPC

India's largest power generator; thermal, hydro and renewable plant O&M workforce

Current technology managementIntegrated Annual Report 2024-25: enhancing digitalisation across operations, "from predictive maintenance to AI-driven planning"; NETRA is its R&D arm.

Source: NTPC Integrated Annual Report 2024-25 (BSE filing, 7 Aug 2025) · checked 11 Oct 2026

Adani Green Energy

Large solar and wind operator with field O&M teams across many states

Current technology managementIntegrated Annual Report 2024-25: Energy Network Operation Centre (ENOC) in Ahmedabad monitors operating assets across 12 states in real time, with predictive maintenance, real-time corrective-action suggestions and an industrial cloud partnership with Google for ML/AI.

Source: Adani Green Energy Integrated Annual Report 2024-25 — ENOC · checked 11 Oct 2026

Suzlon Energy

Wind-turbine maker with a national service (O&M) network

Current technology managementOfficial services page: integrated O&M with "predictive analytics, AI automation and digital performance monitoring"; SCADA and digital tools for asset performance.

Source: Suzlon — project & service solutions · checked 11 Oct 2026

With AI / For AI

With AI

Drones, thermal cameras and monitoring centres spot hot spots and line faults; the technician still climbs, isolates and repairs. Tata Power and Adani Green report AI-led predictive maintenance (see leaders below). NEST view

For AI

Data centres need grid connections, substations, solar and battery storage. Field electricians and linemen build and maintain them. NEST view
