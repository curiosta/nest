Aviation & rail MRO track

A regulated sub-track inside this domain.

  • A DGCA-licensed aircraft maintenance engineer (AME) must sign the certificate of release to service, so the legal accountability stays with a person.
  • MRO facilities grew from 96 (2014) to 166 (2025). Source: MoCA Annual Report 2025
  • Rail: RRB CEN 02/2025 lists 6,238 technician vacancies (fitter, welder, electrical, S&T and more). Source: RRB CEN 02/2025
  • Fairs: the Wings India 2026 Aviation Job Fair (30 Jan 2026, Begumpet, Hyderabad) is on the calendar. Most AME hiring still runs through AME schools, walk-ins and campuses.

Demand signals

  • SIAM: about 2 lakh EV-skilled people needed by 2030 for a 30% EV-adoption target. Source: Economic Times / SIAM
  • India Cooling Action Plan: target of 1 lakh certified RAC service technicians. Source: MoEFCC Ozone Cell
  • Telecom SSC: telecom talent gap of 2.41 million, projected to widen to 2030. Source: TSSC
