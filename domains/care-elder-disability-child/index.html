🧓 Domain

Care: elder, disability & child

Lifting, bathing, feeding, comforting and keeping someone safe need a trusted person in the room. Apps can remind and monitor; they cannot hold a hand or notice that a child is frightened.

New on 11 Oct 2026: split out of the former “Hospitality & care frontline” domain, which is now Hospitality & food-service frontline.

Demand signals

  • UNFPA India Ageing Report 2023: people aged 60+ reach 347 million (20.8%) by 2050, up from 149 million in 2022. Source: UNFPA India
  • EAC-PM draft care-economy working paper (2026): domestic care-worker demand above 30 million by 2050, about 15.6 million of it for eldercare. A draft, quoted as such. Source: EAC-PM draft paper
