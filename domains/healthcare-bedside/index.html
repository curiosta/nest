Healthcare bedside & allied

Bedside care, procedures, and physical presence with patients cannot be fully agentic. Regulation, empathy under stress, and hands-on technique keep humans in the loop until robots reliably replace boring or dangerous tasks.

Industry leaders · tech management

How listed employers currently run ops tech, automation, safety systems, and workforce skilling for this physical domain. Notes prefer official strategy / annual-report / IR / careers pages. TBC means not verified this pass — we never invent.

Apollo Hospitals

Large private hospital network; major bedside & allied employer

Current technology managementOfficial digital programme: Azure cloud migration of hospital platforms, AI-enabled EMR (risk scoring, GenAI clinical summaries), Doctors’ App for ward rounds/PACS, NABH Digital accreditation, connected care (wearables, 5G ambulances), and an in-house SOC / MDR cybersecurity stack — tech supports clinicians rather than replacing bedside care.

Source: Apollo — Digital Transformation and AI for Better Care · checked 6 Oct 2026

Narayana Health

Major hospital chain with high-volume inpatient & allied staffing

Current technology managementInvestor/digital disclosures: cloud-first stack with ATHMA (hospital OS), MEDHA analytics/AI, NH Care patient app, AADI (doctors) and NAMAH (nursing) apps; EMR expansion, digital booking/kiosks (~70% appointments digital in one FY update), AI bots for coding/helpdesk/claims, and MAR medication workflows — bedside roles remain central with digital assist.

Source: Narayana Health digital / investor materials (ATHMA–MEDHA) · checked 6 Oct 2026

Fortis Healthcare

Large multi-specialty hospital network employing nurses, technicians and ward teams

Current technology managementFY25–26 annual report: EMR rollout across OPD/IPD facilities, AI-enabled solutions and data platforms, workflow automation, cybersecurity investment, plus clinical tech (robotic surgery systems, advanced CT, AI-enabled GI endoscopy) — digitalisation of clinical workflows alongside physical care delivery.

Source: Fortis Healthcare Annual Report FY 2025-26 · checked 6 Oct 2026

Max Healthcare

Major North India hospital group with bedside & allied hiring

Current technology managementIntegrated reporting: doctor/patient apps, digital inpatient services, appointment and nursing-roster software, data lake, WhatsApp chatbot for scheduling/video consults; earlier AR also documented web EHR rollout and AI radiology assist (e.g. Qure) — ops tech layered on physical hospital work.

Source: Max Healthcare integrated annual report (digital initiatives) · checked 6 Oct 2026
