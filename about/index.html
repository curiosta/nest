About NEST

How our domains map to official frameworks

NEST groups work into 22 physical-work domains in plain language. These are our own groups, not an official classification. India's official frameworks cut the same work differently: the National Qualification Register (NQR) lists qualifications and the body that awards each one, and each qualification file names the matching code in the National Classification of Occupations 2015 (NCO-2015).

How we verify fairs

NEST lists job fairs only — not individual vacancies. Every card must have an official URL we can check.

Verification levels

  • Gov — Listed on NCS, state employment / e-Rozgar, ministry, or PIB with a durable official page.
  • NSDC — NSDC Rozgar Mela / JobX / SSC-announced programme pages.
  • Campus — University, ITI, or polytechnic official placement notice.
  • Industry — CII, FICCI, ASSOCHAM, or sector chamber official page.
  • Private-verified — Named organiser + public registration page + evidence of recurrence (or a clear first-edition official page). Flyer-only fails.

Date honesty

  • Exact dates for upcoming editions only when officially announced.
  • Exact dates for past 2026 editions when backed by official pages and/or durable news corroboration (e.g. PIB, state portals, organiser site + PTI).
  • Otherwise “Usually …” or “Not yet announced · Last: …” for recurring hubs.
  • We never invent dates. Past cards show a Past badge when the start date is before today.

Physical-domain rule

  • Fairs need not be physical-only. Mixed multi-sector events are OK.
  • They must include physical-domain jobs (bedside, trades, floor, field, agri/food, hospitality/food service, emergency/safety, wet-lab, live-events hardware, energy & utilities field, care, repair & field maintenance, security/defence/policing, teaching & hands-on training, sports/fitness/wellness, mining/maritime/offshore, survey & geospatial field, animal care & veterinary, home/sanitation/waste services, craft & heritage trades, data centres & cooling, electronics/chips/robotics). Desk-only hiring events are out unless they hire for Human in the Loop roles, such as data annotation or AI review; those still need an official page.

Drop rules

  • Flyer / WhatsApp-only with no official URL
  • News article without an organiser page
  • Desk-only hiring events with no Human in the Loop roles (software, pure remote knowledge work)
  • Individual job listings (out of scope for this calendar)

Propose a fair

Send Manoj or Manasi (NEST's founders) the official page URL, city, dates, organiser type, and which physical domains it serves. Soft submissions without an official URL will be declined.

See also calendar/sources.md.

What's out

NEST is opinionated. If an agent can finish the work at a desk with no human check, it does not belong on this site — even if a fair mentions it.

  • Software engineering / “full stack” desk roles
  • Pure data analysis / BI without field ops
  • SEO, content mills, prompt-only “AI jobs”
  • Prompt engineering and other laptop roles where no person checks or signs off the AI's output
  • Remote-only tutoring / freelance writing
  • Call-centre scripting without field service
  • Desk-only design / PM with no physical build
  • Generic “digital marketing” WFH listings
  • Crypto / trading desk roles

Borderline cases (e.g. field service with a digital ticket system) stay in when the core work is physical. When unsure, we ask: would an agent at a laptop finish the job with no human check?

Physical work that AI creates — data-centre power and cooling, robot and drone maintenance, field data collection — is in: see Work for AI.

Human in the Loop roles are in, at a desk or in the field: data labelling and annotation, content moderation, AI output review, clinical and legal review of AI, fraud and claims review, robot teleoperation, drone piloting, autonomous-vehicle safety operation, on-site AI quality inspection and warehouse exception handling. The test: does a person supervise, correct, approve or take over from the AI? See Human in the Loop.
