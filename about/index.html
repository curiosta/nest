About NEST

NEST Collective is a WhatsApp community: Network for Education, Science & Technology. This site, nest.curiosta.com, is the public window for AI-resilient physical careers and verified job fairs.

People

  • Manoj Sahukar — owner
  • Manasi — admin

WhatsApp community

NEST runs as WhatsApp community groups for education, science and technology members. This draft does not publish private invite links. Ask Manoj or Manasi to add you.

What we publish

  • Eight physical domains with mind maps and skill ladders
  • A verified fair calendar (fairs only — no job board)
  • Clear verification rules and an exclusions list

Part of the Curiosta family of static sites (same freeze → GitHub Pages workflow as Kinetic).
