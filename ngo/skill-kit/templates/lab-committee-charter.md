# Lab committee charter

The IBT guidelines ask each school to set up a committee of the headmaster,
sarpanch, SHG chairperson, an advocate, a press reporter and a community
entrepreneur (https://myibtschool.vigyanashram.in/uploads/VA-IBT-SOP.pdf).

- **Lab name and location:**
- **Route:** IBT in a school / DBRT-style course / community lab
- **Members:** headmaster; sarpanch; SHG chairperson; advocate; press reporter;
  community entrepreneur; NGO representative; a parent; a learner representative
  (over 18)
- **Purpose:** oversee safety, safeguarding, money and the service menu; link
  the lab to village needs.
- **Meets:** monthly; minutes kept.
- **Decides:** service menu and price list; who may do paid work (adults, or
  cases cleared by a labour lawyer); spending above an agreed limit;
  complaints.
- **Reviews each quarter:** KPI tracker, service log, incidents, tool register,
  funding tracker.
- **Safeguarding lead:** name. **Complaints contact:** role (not a personal
  number in public documents).
- **Conflicts of interest:** members declare any business with the lab.
- Adopted on (date), signed by members.
