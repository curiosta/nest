# Parent consent and safeguarding (draft: get a lawyer's review before use)

## Safeguarding policy (summary)
- A named safeguarding lead; a child-protection policy; POCSO and POSH
  committees, as Odisha's 2025 school vocational contract requires of training
  partners (see references/sources.md, OSEPA EoI).
- Two adults present with learners under 18; no one-to-one closed-door
  sessions.
- Background checks for instructors and volunteers.
- No photos or names of children published without written parental consent.
- **Under-14s: learning tasks only, never paid work.**
- **14 to 18:** no Schedule processes (welding, flammable gas and similar)
  outside supervised curriculum practice; no paid work unless a labour lawyer
  has given a written opinion that the arrangement is lawful.
