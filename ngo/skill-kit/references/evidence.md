# Evidence and honest economics

The "school as a production centre" idea is real: students install drip
irrigation, rent out sprayers, make tree guards and poultry cages, build soak
pits and test well water (IBT SOP:
https://myibtschool.vigyanashram.in/uploads/VA-IBT-SOP.pdf).
**The published figures do not show service income covering running costs.**

| Figure (each source's own) | Year | Source |

| Services by an IBT school: about Rs 40,000 a year (value) | 2016 | same deck |

"Value of services" is not profit. No independent cost-recovery study found.

## Outcomes reported (mostly self-reported)
- Vigyan Ashram: IBT in "140+ schools in Maharashtra benefiting over 15,000
  students" (2022-23 report).
  https://vigyanashram.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/2022-23.pdf
- Lend-A-Hand India: MSFC adopted by 600+ secondary schools; 56% of MSFC
  students go on to further vocational education vs 20% of others. The chapter
  notes its evidence is self-reported and calls for a controlled study.
  https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-93951-9_2
- UP "Learning by Doing" (UNICEF, Samagra Shiksha UP, Vigyan Ashram, STARS
  Forum): labs in 1,772 schools plus 570 PM SHRI schools in 2024-25; 2,160
  teachers trained.
  https://vigyanashram.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Learning-by-Doing-LBD-Report-2021-25.pdf

## KPIs
- Samagra Shiksha vocational KPIs: enrolment (girls, children with special
  needs), retention, labs equipped, practical-to-theory hours, field visits,
  pass with a vocational subject, progression, placement or apprenticeship.
  https://samagrashiksha.in/download/AWP&B/norms.pdf (`To confirm`: copy)
- Vigyan Ashram model: number and value of services, Class 10 results, share
  going on to technical courses.
- NEST additions: instructor retention, service income vs running cost, safety
  incidents (target zero), alumni enterprises within a year.
