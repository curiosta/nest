# Space, tools and safety

## Space and utilities (IBT SOP)
Two classrooms (800 to 1,000 sq ft), a quarter acre of farm land (can be
rented), an electricity connection and a water connection, time in the school
timetable. Source: https://myibtschool.vigyanashram.in/uploads/VA-IBT-SOP.pdf

## Tools (MSFC Level 1 "suggestive" list, Govt of Maharashtra 2015, in the SOP)
The list says to buy only basic tools so routine tasks can be practised. It
also contains items from other trades (refrigeration gear, a foreign electrical
code): trim it to your service menu.
- Engineering: hammers, saws, chisels, files, vice, clamps; steel rule,
  vernier caliper, spirit level, plumb bob; hand drill; arc welding machine;
  pipe cutter, bender, wrench; PPE (gloves, mask, apron).
- Energy & environment: multimeter, clamp meter, wire strippers, crimpers,
  MCB/RCCB, earthing set, non-contact voltage detector, rubber matting,
  inverter, battery, solar lights; models of a biogas plant and soak pit.
- Agriculture & animal husbandry: hoes, spades, pruners, grafting and budding
  knives, soil testing kit, soil auger, shade net, polybags, watering cans,
  wheelbarrow, weighing balance; protective clothing (apron, mask, gloves,
  boots).
- Home & health: food-processing utensils, pressure cooker, thermometer,
  sewing kit.
Source: https://myibtschool.vigyanashram.in/uploads/VA-IBT-SOP.pdf

## Safety rules
- Each MSFC unit includes safe use per manufacturer's instructions, PPE and a
  workshop tour covering the electric board and first aid (SOP, above).
- Electrical: CEA (Measures relating to Safety and Electric Supply)
  Regulations 2023, regulation 31: installation work by a State-licensed
  electrical contractor under a certificate-of-competency holder, done by a
  State permit holder, with narrow exceptions (replacing lamps, fans, fuses,
  switches, small appliances). Gazette text checked 12 Oct 2026.
  https://indiacode.ecourtsindia.com/rules/4856ff62/
  Paid house wiring for villagers goes through a licensed contractor.
- Food sold to the public: FSSAI registration for turnover up to Rs 1.5 crore
  (from 1 Apr 2026).
  https://fssai.gov.in/upload/advisories/2026/03/69b4054bb6cd6Order%20dated%2013thMarch2026%5FRevised%20Turnover%20threshold.pdf
- Child and adolescent labour: see compliance.md.

## NEST suggestions (not rules)
- A written risk check per activity (templates/risk-assessment.md), posted at
  the station.
- First-aid kit and a trained first-aider; fire extinguisher near welding; eye
  protection for grinding and welding; RCCB on the lab supply.
- Tool register and issue log (the SOP lists tool pilferage as a challenge).
