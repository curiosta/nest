# Routes: DBRT, IBT, or a community lab

DBRT and IBT are **separate programmes**. Pick one per group of learners.

## The Vigyan Ashram model
Vigyan Ashram (Pabal, Pune district; started 1983 by Dr S. S. Kalbag) follows
"Rural Development through Education System": learning while doing, multi-skill
training, the school as a production centre (students provide services that the
community pays for) and the instructor as an entrepreneur.
Sources: https://vigyanashram.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/DBRT-Broucher-English.pdf ,
https://vigyanashram.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/VA-IBT-Program-Presentation.pdf

Four areas: Engineering; Energy & Environment; Home & Health; Agriculture &
Animal Husbandry. Computers, design tools and AI tools are now part of DBRT.
Source: https://vigyanashram.com/education/diploma-in-basic-rural-technology-dbrt/

## DBRT: diploma for youth
- Certified by NIOS. Entry 8th pass; 600 hours; four papers (452 to 455): Our
  Health, Agriculture & Animal Husbandry, Rural Engineering, Our Home
  Environment; each 30 theory + 50 practical + 20 internal.
  https://rchyderabad.nios.ac.in/page/diploma-in-basic-rural-technology-452-455.html
- At Pabal: one-year residential course, ages 16 to 22, then an apprenticeship
  year. https://vigyanashram.com/education/diploma-in-basic-rural-technology-dbrt/
- To award the NIOS diploma you must be, or partner with, a NIOS Accredited
  Vocational Institution (AVI); for NGOs this includes society/trust
  registration and 3+ years of existence.
  `To confirm`: the NIOS site timed out; current AVI norms and fees not checked.
  https://nios.ac.in/departmentsunits/student-support-services/accreditation.aspx
- Existing DBRT centres: Loksadhana (https://loksadhana.org/skilling/),
  Govardhan Skill Centre (https://govardhanskillcentre.com/diploma-in-basic-rural-technology/),
  Narmadalaya VIRT (https://www.virt-narmada.com/dbrt).

## IBT: a subject inside a school
- Pre-vocational subject for Classes 8 to 10, one day a week (about 10
  periods), started in 3 schools in 1987.
  https://vigyanashram.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/VA-IBT-Program-Presentation.pdf
- Recognised under NSQF as the Multi-Skill Foundation Course (MSFC), Levels 1
  and 2. https://myibtschool.vigyanashram.in/uploads/VA-IBT-SOP.pdf
- Maharashtra SSC board examines it in Class 10; CBSE offers a Multi Skill
  Foundation Course (code 416).
  https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-93951-9_2
- The school commits for at least 3 years, sets up the lab and selects
  instructors to Vigyan Ashram's guidelines; Vigyan Ashram trains and monitors.
  https://myibtschool.vigyanashram.in/uploads/VA-IBT-SOP.pdf

## Community lab without a diploma (NEST suggestion)
Run multi-skill sessions and services; learners later take an RPL or PMKVY
assessment at an accredited centre for an NSQF certificate.

## Launch checklist (NEST synthesis)
1. Route and learners.
2. Legal shell (trust, society or Section 8 company); Income-tax registration
   (see compliance.md).
3. Partners: school, Gram Panchayat; lab committee (headmaster, sarpanch, SHG
   chairperson, advocate, press reporter, community entrepreneur, per the IBT
   SOP); ask Vigyan Ashram or STARS Forum about training.
4. Asset map: rooms, land, power, water; artisans and certificates.
5. Three-year budget from real quotes only.
6. Instructor plan: four, at least one a woman (IBT SOP); RPL; honorarium.
7. Safety and safeguarding (space-tools-safety.md, compliance.md), and a labour
   lawyer's written opinion before paid student work.
8. First service menu, price list and order log.
9. KPIs from day one (evidence.md).
