# Funding routes (checked 12 Oct 2026 IST)

**Warning:** NABARD has publicly warned that posts advertising "grants for
NGOs" under its SDERY scheme are misleading and that it issued no such
announcement. Use only nabard.org or the NABARD Regional Office. Never pay
anyone to "process" a grant.
https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nabard_nabard-publicadvisory-fakealert-activity-7447531344424816640-FMRr

| Route | What it can pay for | Who can apply, and the catch |
|---|---|---|
| CSR (Companies Act s.135, Schedule VII) | education incl. employment-enhancing vocational skills; livelihood enhancement; rural development projects | the company decides; MCA says Schedule VII items are read liberally. Most realistic route for a new lab. https://indialawacts.in/assets/pdf/companies/Companies-%20Schedule%20VII.pdf , https://www.csrbox.org/media/2.-MCA-General-Circular-No.-21-2014-Dated-18-June-2014.pdf |
| VB-G RAM G (replaced MGNREGA on 1 Jul 2026) | permissible works include training-cum-skill development centres, youth centres, additional classrooms and laboratories in government schools | buildings and assets only, through the Gram Panchayat plan approved by the Gram Sabha; not salaries or tools. https://www.pib.gov.in/PressNoteDetails.aspx?ModuleId=3&NoteId=158510&lang=2&reg=48 , https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2319426&lang=1&reg=3 |
| PMKVY 4.0 / RPL | short NSQF courses and RPL assessment | providers accredited for the job role and on Skill India Digital. `To confirm`: from a summary of the guidelines. https://www.msde.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-11/PMKVY%204.0%20Guidelines.pdf |
| NABARD | skill development and entrepreneurship for rural youth | outcome-linked. `To confirm`: nabard.org timed out; eligibility from a search summary. https://www.nabard.org/content.aspx?id=478 |
| Samagra Shiksha (school vocational education) | up to Rs 5 lakh per school per job role for tools and equipment; recurring Rs 12 to 24 lakh per job role by enrolment; Rs 3,000 per student a year transport (spoke to hub) | `To confirm`: norms from a copy, not the ministry site. Money flows to state-selected training partners; Odisha 2025 bar: 5 years in skilling, Rs 4 crore average turnover, 1,000 trained. https://samagrashiksha.in/download/AWP&B/norms.pdf , OSEPA EoI (see sources.md) |
| Odisha: OSDA / Nua Odisha | placement-linked NSQF training | for established training partners. `To confirm`: 2025 eligibility (5 years of operation) seen on an aggregator copy. https://sdte.odisha.gov.in/node/174860 |
| DST SEED | TARA: long-term core support (5 to 15 years) for S&T NGOs as rural field labs; SUNIL: NGO + knowledge-institution projects (Vigyan Ashram with SPPU: Rs 93.6 lakh, 2023-25) | TARA needs 10 years of rural technology field experience; SUNIL calls are periodic. Partner with an existing grantee. https://www.indiascienceandtechnology.gov.in/programme-schemes/research-and-development/technological-advancement-rural-areas-tara , https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2150130 |

NEST suggestion: building from the Panchayat plan; tools and three years of
running costs from CSR; certificates through an accredited PMKVY/RPL centre.
**Applications, budgets and signatures are human decisions. Stop and ask.**
