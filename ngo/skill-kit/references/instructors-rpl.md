# Instructors: local artisans and RPL

- **Model:** instructors are village entrepreneurs with demonstrable skills;
  local skilled youth are trained as instructors; a science or maths teacher
  teaches theory.
  https://vigyanashram.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/VA-IBT-Program-Presentation.pdf
- An instructor can run their own business from the school workshop with
  students helping.
  http://yogeshkulkarniva.blogspot.com/2015/02/keys-to-success-of-skills-training-in.html
- **Qualification (NSQF MSFC curriculum):** ITI certificate, diploma or degree
  in a relevant field, HSC-vocational, DBRT, or RPL Level 3 or above. An
  uncertified artisan can qualify through RPL.
  https://myibtschool.vigyanashram.in/uploads/VA-IBT-SOP.pdf
- **Numbers:** four instructors from the community, at least one a woman (IBT
  SOP, above).
- **Training (2016):** two 6-day programmes at Pabal for 4 instructors per
  school; a further 1-month course at extra cost if basic technical skills are
  missing. Ask Vigyan Ashram for current terms.
  https://vigyanashram.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/VA-IBT-Program-Presentation.pdf
- **Known problems:** time to build instructor confidence; keeping instructors
  who are not paid the honorarium they need (SOP, above). Lend-A-Hand India moved
  from community instructors to vocationally certified staff as it scaled.
  https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-93951-9_2
- **Government-funded school courses:** Odisha's 2025 vocational training
  partner EoI needs degree- or diploma-qualified trainers aged 18 to 45,
  budgeted at Rs 20,000 a month each; artisans fit as guest lecturers.
  https://osepa.odisha.gov.in/webadmin/upload/EOI%20FOR%20SELECTION%20OF%20VOCATIONAL%20TRAINING%20PARTNERS%20FOR%20IMPLEMENTATION%20OF%20VOCATIONAL%20EDUCATION%20IN%20ODISHA_10_38_34ambf7adeebf2419ad112bc8e364fc8c763.pdf

NEST suggestion: pair each artisan with a qualified coordinator; pay a fixed
honorarium plus a share of service income; enrol them for RPL.
**Hiring and pay are human decisions. Stop and ask.**
