# Compliance (not legal advice; checked 12 Oct 2026 IST)

## Income tax (the old 12A and 80G)
From 1 Apr 2026 the Income-tax Act 2025 applies: registration as a Registered
Non-Profit Organisation under section 332 (old 12A/12AB) and approval under
section 354 for donors' deduction (old 80G). Both via Form No. 105; orders in
Form No. 107. Regular registration: 5 tax years, or 10 if income was at most
Rs 5 crore in each of the two previous years.
https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/form-105-faqs
Existing registrations continue until they expire. `To confirm`: from a
secondary explainer.
https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/section-332-transition-12a-12ab-to-rnpo-it-act-2025

## FCRA (foreign contributions)
Registration for associations operating at least 3 years, or prior permission
for a defined project; all foreign money first into the FCRA account at SBI New
Delhi Main Branch; at most 20% on administration; annual return FC-4;
registration lasts 5 years; 2026 rules print purposes and states on the
certificate and need at least Rs 10 lakh used over two years for renewal.
https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2287845&lang=1&reg=3
Registration also asks for Rs 15 lakh spent on core activities over three
years. `To confirm`: 2019 FAQ, may have changed.
https://fcraonline.nic.in/home/PDF_Doc/fc_faq_07062019.pdf

## Child and adolescent labour
No child (under 14, or the RTE age) may be employed or permitted to work in
any occupation, except helping the family or a family enterprise after school or
in vacations, or as an artist. No adolescent (14 to 18) may work in Schedule
occupations and processes (mines; inflammable substances or explosives;
hazardous processes as in the Factories Act).
https://judicialacademy.nic.in/sites/default/files/1482211737_child-labour-amendment-act-2016.pdf
A 2017 notification expanded the Schedule.
https://indiacode.ecourtsindia.com/rules/amended-schedule-to-child-and-adolescent-labour-prohibition-and-7006973c/
The amended Act has no stated exemption for school training. `To confirm`: how
it treats paid "production centre" work by students is a legal question.
**Get a labour lawyer's written opinion before students do paid work.**
