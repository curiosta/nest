Human in the Loop

Desk review roles

Some desk jobs stay human because a person has to check the AI. These five roles sit at a desk or online, not in the field. They are on NEST because the person supervises, corrects, approves or overrides the AI. Desk work an agent can finish with no human check stays out.

Every link was checked on the page on 22 Oct 2026. Quotes are given only where the official text was opened; everything else is a summary with a link. No pay figures and no job ads: NEST is fairs only. Field and floor HITL roles, such as drone pilots and robot operators, are on the Human in the Loop list and on each domain page.

  • Data labelling and annotation
  • Content moderation
  • AI output review and evaluation

Data labelling and annotation

Desk or remote; also in labs at ITIs and polytechnics

Models learn from labelled examples, and someone has to make and check those labels, especially in Indian languages. NEST view

What they do Labels images, video, text, speech or sensor data so models can learn, and corrects labels the model got wrong. NEST view

Evidence

  • Lok Sabha answer (22 Jul 2026): 27 IndiaAI Data and AI Labs at NIELIT centres are operational; another 188 at ITIs and polytechnics are underway
  • PIB: IndiaAI courses for Data Annotator and Data Curator roles, taught in Data Labs in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities
  • iMerit lists data-enrichment centres including Ranchi, Shillong and Hubballi
  • NextWealth describes its annotation as a human-in-the-loop approach
  • DesiCrew builds and checks language data across 22+ Indian languages
  • NITI Frontier Tech: Karya pays rural workers for language data work in their own languages

Routes in NCVET-recognised course “Fundamentals of Data Annotation using Python” (120 hours) at IndiaAI Data and AI Labs; Fundamentals of Data Annotation using Python (NQR, NIELIT, NSQF level 3.5), valid till 18 Feb 2028; Application of Data Annotation in Agriculture (NQR, NIELIT, NSQF level 3.5), valid till 18 Feb 2028

Content moderation

Desk or remote

Platforms flag content automatically, but the IT Rules require large platforms to keep human oversight of those tools, and the hard calls need a person. NEST view

What they do Reviews posts, images and videos that automated filters flag, and decides whether they break the rules. NEST view

Evidence

  • IT Rules 2021, Rule 4(4): human oversight of automated tools on large platforms
  • The Guardian (5 Feb 2026): moderators in rural India classify content flagged by automated systems, and describe the toll of the work

Routes in No formal course found yet. To confirm.

Before you start: The Guardian reports moderators describing the toll of watching abusive content. Ask about rest breaks, rotation and counselling. NEST view

AI output review and evaluation

Desk or remote

Someone has to judge whether an AI answer or robot clip is right before it is used to train the next version. NEST view

What they do Rates and corrects AI answers, images or robot clips against a rubric, so the next version improves. Knowing the trade, such as nursing or wiring, makes the review better. NEST view

Evidence

  • CNBC (Jun 2026): Indian firms that recruit people to record task videos for robots also check quality parameters and annotate data
  • MIT Technology Review (1 Apr 2026): Micro1 task videos are reviewed by AI and a human

Legal and judicial review of AI

Desk, in courts and law offices

Courts are starting to write the human check into policy. Kerala High Court's policy for district courts keeps findings and orders with judges and has AI output verified by people. NEST view

What they do Checks AI-drafted translations, summaries or research before anyone relies on them; the decision stays with the judge or lawyer. NEST view

Evidence

  • LiveLaw (19 Jul 2025): Kerala High Court policy says AI translations must be verified by judges or qualified translators, and the human verification process must be logged

Routes in A law or translation qualification. To confirm which courses cover AI review.

Fraud and claims review

Desk, in banks, lenders and insurers

An RBI committee says lenders should stay liable for what their AI decides, and that final authority should rest with people who can override it. NEST view

What they do Reviews transactions, loans or claims that an AI model flags or declines, and can override it. NEST view

Evidence

  • RBI FREE-AI report: humans keep final authority and can override AI; human-in-the-loop reviews as a fallback when models fail
