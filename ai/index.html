With AI · For AI

Work with AI, work for AI.

AI is not only a desk story. Electricians, nurses, farmers and technicians already use AI tools on the job — and AI itself needs people with tools in their hands: data-centre technicians, robot and drone maintainers, field data collectors, sensor, EV and solar crews.

1 · Working with AI

The tool changes; the hands-on part stays. Examples cite a source; lines marked NEST view are our plain description of typical work, not a sourced claim.

⚡

Electrician & line technician

AI on the jobSmart-meter data, fault-finding and thermal-imaging apps, digital permit-to-work checklists, AR wiring manuals on a phone.

Stays humanIsolation, testing, terminations and the final safety call.

ExampleSmart meters are field hardware: PIB reports 4.69 crore installed under RDSS and 6.13 crore across all schemes (30 Mar 2026). Each one is fitted, commissioned and replaced by a person on site.[10]

🩺

Nurse & community health worker

AI on the jobAI screening aids, decision support in teleconsultation, digital charting.

Stays humanTaking the image or sample properly, examining the patient, explaining, referring.

ExampleMoHFW reports MadhuNetrAI helping non-specialist health workers screen for diabetic retinopathy, a ‘Cough Against TB’ AI tool used in community screening, and decision support in eSanjeevani.[12]

🌾

Farmer & agri-service provider

AI on the jobVoice chatbots in local languages, spraying drones, crop and weather advisories, soil and moisture sensors.

Stays humanField judgement, timing, handling chemicals safely, flying within the rules.

ExampleKisan e-Mitra, a voice AI chatbot for PM-KISAN, answers in 11 languages.[13] Namo Drone Didi supplies drones to women SHGs with 15 days of pilot training and 5 days of drone-assistant training.[9]

🏭

Factory & maintenance technician

AI on the jobVision inspection, predictive-maintenance alerts, cobots at the line, AR/VR work instructions.

Stays humanDiagnosing the real fault, changeovers, lock-out/tag-out, fixing the machine.

ExampleTata Motors reports 1,560+ AI safety cameras and predictive-maintenance pilots;[16] Mahindra cites Vision AI, VR–AR process models and a ‘Maintenance GPT’.[17]

🚚

Logistics & last-mile crew

AI on the jobRoute-optimisation apps, handheld scanners, AI-assisted sorting, EV telematics.

Stays humanLoading, driving, doorstep handover, handling damage and exceptions.

ExampleNEST view — routing software plans the day; the van, the parcel and the customer are still physical.

🏗️

Construction & site crew

AI on the jobDrone surveys, sensor readings, digital snag and safety checklists, model-based (BIM) drawings on tablets.

Stays humanSetting out, fixing, inspecting, working safely at height.

ExampleNEST view — software flags clashes; a mason, fitter or electrician still resolves them on site.

2 · Working for AI

Physical jobs that exist because AI needs power, cooling, cables, machines and real-world data. Every number below links to its source; we never invent figures.

🔌

Data-centre electrical technician

UPS, switchgear, busbars, generators and power distribution for server halls that run 24×7.

Demand signalIndia's operational data-centre capacity reached 1.6 GW in 2025, with 322.4 MW under construction and more than 8 GW committed and planned (Knight Frank).[1] The sector employs about 86,000–90,000 people; cooling and electrical roles are projected to grow more than 100% through 2029 (Quess).[3]

India skill pathITI Electrician (DGT CTS)[18] → apprenticeship at a facility → shift technician. ITI Information & Communication Technology System Maintenance (NSQF 4) for the IT side.[22]

❄️

Data-centre cooling / HVAC technician

Chillers, CRAH units, liquid-cooling loops, pumps and leak detection.

Demand signalQuess projects cooling and electrical facilities roles to grow more than 100% through 2029.[3] Cushman & Wakefield counts 2,887 MW of upcoming supply by 2030.[2]

India skill pathITI Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technician (2 years)[18] → chiller-plant shift technician → BMS operator.

🧵

Fibre & structured-cabling technician

Splicing, testing and labelling fibre and copper inside and between data halls.

Demand signalEvery new hall in the pipeline above needs cabling crews.[1]

India skill pathTSSC Optical Fiber Splicer QP TEL/Q6400 (NSQF 3)[24]; ITI Fiber To Home Technician[18]; TSSC ICT Technician QP, which names cloud and data-centre equipment.[23]

🦾

Robot & cobot maintainer

Installing, teaching, calibrating and repairing industrial robots and cobots.

Demand signalIndia installed a record 9,120 industrial robots in 2024 (+7%), sixth in the world; operational stock reached 52,570 units (IFR).[7]

India skill pathITI Technician Mechatronics or Industrial Robotics & Digital Manufacturing Technician (2 years each)[18] → OEM service engineer.

🛩️

Drone pilot & drone maintainer

Flying survey, spraying and inspection drones; batteries, motors, propellers and repairs.

Demand signal251 DGCA-authorised RPTOs have trained 42,412 remote pilots; 25,791 people were trained in drone job roles under PMKVY by 31 Dec 2025 (PIB, 25 Mar 2026).[8]

India skill pathITI Drone Technician (6 months, NSQF 3)[19] or Drone Pilot (Junior)[18]; DGCA Remote Pilot Certificate through an authorised RPTO.[8]

📍

Field data collector & annotation-in-the-field

Standing at the plot, site or asset to capture photos, readings and labels that AI systems learn from.

Demand signalAgriStack's Digital Crop Survey app requires the surveyor to be at the field location,[14] and uses geofencing and AI-based image processing to check the data.[15]

India skill pathNo dedicated NSQF qualification found this pass (TBC). Surveyors are usually appointed by state revenue / agriculture departments — check your district office.

📡

Sensor & IoT installer

Fitting, configuring and maintaining meters, sensors and gateways that feed AI and analytics.

Demand signalRDSS has sanctioned 20.33 crore smart meters; 4.69 crore were installed under the scheme by 30 Mar 2026.[10]

India skill pathITI IoT Technician (Smart City) (1 year, NSQF 3.5)[20] or IoT Technician (Smart Agriculture)[18]; TSSC Telecom Technician – IoT Devices/Systems QP.[23]

🔋

EV & battery technician

Battery packs, BMS, high-voltage safety, chargers and vehicle repair.

Demand signal2.3 million EVs were sold in India in 2025; 52,718 public charging stations were available as of July 2026, 16,561 of them fast (PIB).[11]

India skill pathITI Mechanic Electric Vehicle (2 years, NSQF 4) covering battery/BMS and high-voltage systems.[21]

☀️

Solar technician (incl. solar for data centres)

Building and maintaining rooftop and utility solar that powers homes and data centres.

Demand signalCtrlS built a 125 MWp captive solar farm to power 60% of its Mumbai data-centre campus;[4] Equinix brought 26.4 MWp of group-captive solar online for its Indian sites on 1 Nov 2025.[5] PM Surya Ghar has 34,219 registered vendors and has trained over 2.32 lakh people (PIB, 4 Aug 2026).[6]

India skill pathSCGJ Solar PV Installer (Suryamitra) QP SGJ/Q0101[25]; ITI Solar Technician (Electrical).[18]

5 · What's out

Desk-only AI jobs are not on NEST, however popular. They live on the What's out list:

  • Prompt engineering
  • Desk-only data labelling and annotation
  • AI content writing and chatbot scripting
  • Model training, fine-tuning and evaluation at a laptop
  • AI product management with no physical build

The test is the same as everywhere on this site: would an agent at a laptop finish the job? If yes, it is out.
