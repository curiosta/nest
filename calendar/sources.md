# NEST job-fair calendar: sources
Page: `/calendar/`. Generated from `nest-tools/build/fairs.py` (5–6 Oct 2026); phase-2 edits by `nest-tools/build2/phase2_domains.py` (data in `build2/data_p2_fairs.py`), checked 11 Oct 2026.
## Rules
- Exact dates only when the next edition is officially announced.
- Otherwise “Usually …” or “Not yet announced · Last: …”. We never invent dates.
- Calendar of **fairs only** — no individual job listings.
- Include in-person and verified online melas that include physical-domain jobs (mixed multi-sector OK; not digital-desk-only).
- Coverage: verified fairs from Oct 2021 to now, plus upcoming. Exact dates for past editions only when official/news-backed; never invent.
- Recruitment rallies, government appointment-letter events, apprenticeship melas and political-organiser fairs are tagged as such.
- Drop flyer-only, news-without-official-URL, and digital-desk-only hiring events.

## Phase 13 recruitment seasons and calendar layout (12 Oct 2026)
The calendar now mirrors kinetic.curiosta.com/calendar/: a domain x month grid, a month picker, and a Coming up / Already held list with one row per recurring fair.
- **Fairs on record** rows: Peak = verified fairs in that month in 2+ different years; Possible = at least one. National Rozgar Mela appointment-letter ceremonies are not counted.
- **Sourced** rows: only months the source text supports. No source, no marker.
- Recurring series rows (one each): National Rozgar Mela (government appointment letters); PM National Apprenticeship Mela (district editions); Agniveer Army recruitment rallies; DGR ex-servicemen job fairs; NSDC Kaushal Mahotsav (city editions); Namo Maharojgar Melava (Maharashtra); Niyukti mega job fair (Kerala); IYC Mega Job Fair (Indian Youth Congress). Their past editions stay listed under Already held.
- End dates are read from each fair's stated dates (e.g. '7-8 Oct 2026' ends 8 Oct; 'From 26 Sep 2026 (3-day fair)' ends 28 Sep). Never invented.
