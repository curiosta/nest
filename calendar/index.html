04 · About the data

How we built this calendar

Levels it hires for: none of the fair sources in this calendar says which rung of the AI role ladder (L1 to L4) its roles need, so no fair carries a level tag; we do not guess. Go one level up: whatever job you find here, you can learn to direct AI agents with expert-written skills. See the AI role ladder.

Fairs on record rows come from our own verified fairs since October 2021. Peak means we verified fairs in that month in two or more different years. Possible means at least one. National Rozgar Mela appointment-letter ceremonies are listed as fairs but not counted in the bands, because they are not open fairs.

Sourced rows are recruitment seasons that a cited source describes: festive-season hiring for logistics, Agniveer rally schedules, kharif and rabi crop seasons, and engineering campus placements. Where a source gives no month, we leave it blank. Blank always means no data.

  • Economic Times (29 Aug 2025): e-commerce platforms ramp up seasonal hiring ahead of festivities, 3.8 lakh jobs
  • TeamLease (8 Sep 2025): festive hiring eases to 15-20% in 2025 but still adds 2.16 lakh jobs
  • Indian Army zone-wise rally schedule, recruiting year 2025-26 (copy on studyandupdates.in)
  • Indian Army Agniveer rally schedule 2025 (copy on testbook.com)
  • Indian Economic Service, Arthapedia: cropping seasons of India (kharif and rabi)
  • Mint (2 Oct 2025): India campus placements 2026, when institutes place

Fairs show an exact date only when an official page or news report gives one. Status (Upcoming, On now, Ended) is worked out in your browser from today's date, and fairs that are over move to “Already held”. Recurring fairs whose next dates are not announced yet sit under Coming up in their usual month where we know it, marked “Dates not announced yet”, with the last edition we verified.

Organiser types: Gov · NSDC · Campus · Industry · Private-verified. Each fair shows its organiser type; read how we verify fairs.

Every fair, season and dropped item is logged with its source in sources.md. Fairs checked 5–6 Oct 2026; new-domain fairs 11 Oct 2026; seasons checked 12 Oct 2026. Each domain page links straight to its fairs here.
