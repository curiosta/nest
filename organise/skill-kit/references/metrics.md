# Metrics: define them before the fair

Publish your definitions with your numbers. Report each stage separately, and
keep internships and apprenticeships apart from jobs.

| Stage | Definition | How you count it |
|---|---|---|
| Registered | Unique people who completed registration (de-duplicate by phone) | registration export |
| Attended | Registered or walk-in people who entered the venue | gate scans or token issue logs |
| Interviewed | People who had at least one interview | zone token "called" logs or employer sheets |
| Shortlisted | People an employer marked for a next round | employer sheets (written) |
| Written offer | People holding an offer letter or official email naming role, pay, location and joining date | copy or photo of the offer, or employer list |
| **Joined at 30 days** | People confirmed working about 30 days after the fair | follow-up call/SMS plus employer confirmation |
| **Still employed at 90 days** | People confirmed working about 90 days after the fair | follow-up call/SMS plus employer confirmation |

Also track: employers present vs committed, openings committed vs filled,
in-hand pay bands offered, candidates by zone, women and persons with
disabilities (only if voluntarily disclosed), complaints and incidents, and
joining problems per employer.

## Honest reporting rules
- Never mix "offers", "shortlists" and "placements" into one number.
- If you revise a number, publish the old and new number, the date and the reason.
- Say who counted (organiser, employer or government partner) and whether
  anyone checked it independently.
- If a number is unknown, write `TBC`. Don't estimate it.
