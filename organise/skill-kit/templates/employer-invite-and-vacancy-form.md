# Employer invitation and vacancy form

> Draft for the organiser. **The human reviews and sends.** Replace every `{{…}}`.
> Leave `TBC` where something is not yet confirmed. Don't guess.

## Invitation (email or letter)

**Subject:** Invitation to recruit at {{FAIR_NAME}}, {{CITY}}, {{DATE or TBC}}

Dear {{HR_CONTACT_NAME}},

{{ORGANISER_NAME}} is organising **{{FAIR_NAME}}**, a free job fair for
{{AUDIENCE, e.g. ITI, diploma and graduate candidates}} on {{DATE or TBC}} at
{{VENUE or TBC}}, {{CITY}}. {{PARTNERS, if confirmed in writing}}.

We are inviting employers hiring for {{JOB_FAMILIES}}. Stalls are
{{free / ₹X — only if approved by the organiser}}. **Candidates pay nothing.**

To confirm a stall, please send the vacancy form below by {{DEADLINE}}. We publish
a job board for candidates about a week before the fair, so they come prepared
for the roles that suit them.

Conditions of participation:
1. No fee or deposit of any kind may be asked from candidates.
2. Vacancy, pay and location details must be in writing and accurate.
3. Offers made at the fair must be in writing (letterhead or official email).
4. Candidate data shared with you is used only for recruitment to the roles
   listed and deleted when no longer needed for that purpose.
5. You agree to share, in writing, how many candidates you shortlisted, offered
   and (at 30 and 90 days) how many joined.

Regards,
{{SENDER_NAME}}, {{ROLE}}, {{ORGANISER_NAME}} · {{PHONE}} · {{EMAIL}}
