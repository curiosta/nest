# Consent notice (registration)

> Show this as a standalone notice before the form. Plain language; translate it
> into the local language(s) as well. Based on the DPDP Rules, 2025 notice
> elements (see `../references/data-and-consent.md`). Not legal advice.

**Who we are.** {{ORGANISER_NAME}} is organising {{FAIR_NAME}} on {{DATE}}.
Contact for data questions: {{NAME}}, {{EMAIL}}, {{PHONE}}.

**What we collect (itemised).** Name; mobile number; email (optional);
district or locality; highest qualification; trade or stream; years of
experience; job families and zones you choose; where you can work; shift
preference; accessibility needs (optional); preferred arrival slot.

**Why (purpose).**
1. To give you an arrival slot and a zone token for the fair.
2. To share your details **only with employers in the zones you chose**, for the
   roles on the published job board.
3. If you tick the optional box: to contact you about 7, 30 and 90 days after the
   fair to ask whether you got and joined a job, and to help with problems.
4. To produce **anonymous** totals for the post-fair report.

**What we don't do.** We don't charge you. We don't sell or give your data to
anyone else. We don't ask about religion, caste or politics. We don't use your
data for any political or marketing purpose.

**How long we keep it.** Until {{RETENTION_DATE, e.g. 90 days after the fair}}.
After that we delete it or keep only anonymous totals.

**Your choices.** You can withdraw consent as easily as you gave it: {{LINK or
reply STOP to the SMS / WhatsApp number}}. You can ask to see, correct or delete
your data by contacting {{EMAIL/PHONE}}. If you are not satisfied with our
response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

**Fraud warning.** No genuine employer at this fair will ask you for money.
Report fraud on 1930 or at cybercrime.gov.in.
