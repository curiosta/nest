# Candidate slot-registration form

> Build this in any form tool. Show the consent notice (`consent-notice.md`)
> **before** the first question. Collect only these fields. Registration is free.

**{{FAIR_NAME}} · {{DATE}} · {{VENUE}}**
Free registration. Bring {{DOCUMENTS_CHECKLIST, e.g. printed or digital CV, qualification certificates (originals to show, not to hand over)}}.
Walk-ins: a separate lane opens at {{TIME}}, capped at {{N}}.

1. Full name *
2. Mobile number * (used for your slot, token and follow-up calls)
3. Email (optional)
4. District / locality *
5. Highest qualification * (8th / 10th / 12th / ITI / diploma / graduate / postgraduate / other)
6. Trade or stream (for example electrician, fitter, nursing, B.Com)
7. Years of work experience *
8. Job families you're interested in * (tick up to 3; match these to your zones)
   - [ ] Zone A: technical, ITI and field roles
   - [ ] Zone B: retail, sales, logistics and warehouse
   - [ ] Zone C: graduate office roles
   - [ ] {{other zones}}
9. Where can you work? * (home district only / within the state / anywhere in India)
10. Shift preference (day / night / rotational / no preference)
11. Do you need any accessibility support at the venue? (optional, free text)
12. Preferred arrival slot * ({{10:00–11:00}}, {{11:00–12:00}}, …; each slot is capped)
13. Consent *
    - [ ] I have read the notice. I agree that my details may be used to give me a slot and token, and shared **only with employers in the zones I chose**, for recruitment to the roles on the job board.
    - [ ] (optional) Contact me after the fair about how my application went (7, 30 and 90 days).

**Do not add** questions about Aadhaar, caste, religion, political views or affiliation, voter ID or constituency, family income, or photos.

After submission, show and send (once approved): slot time, zone(s), token range or QR, venue map link, the no-fee notice and the helpline.
